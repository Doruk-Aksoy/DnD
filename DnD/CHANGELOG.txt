==== DND NEXT VERSION PATCH NOTES ====

* The Shop in the DnD menu has a new look: a near full screen frame with section tabs along the top (Character, Skills, Gear, Shop, Research, World, Codex), sub tabs below them, and a status strip with your level, experience, credits and budget.
	- Weapons and ammunition are a list with a details pane: hover an entry to see its picture, damage types, properties, description, price and stock. Left click buys, right click sells a weapon or buys ammunition in bulk, as before.
	- Selling a weapon now asks in a popup with Sell and Cancel buttons, and shows how much you get back. A second right click no longer confirms the sale; clicking anywhere outside the buttons cancels it.
	- Slot buttons jump straight to any weapon slot or ammunition category, and slots with several pages have page arrows.
	- Back, home and forward buttons sit at the top right, next to a Close button.
	- The Character and Skills tabs show how many points you have to spend.
	- The Character section has the same look: an Overview with your level, experience, credits and attributes (hover one to read what it does and its refund cost), and a Stat sheet with Offense, Defense, Utility, Exotic and Misc buttons. Long stat lists scroll with the forward and back keys.
	- Perks are on the new frame too: archetype buttons along the top, each tree as a list with lines and arrows from a perk to the perks it unlocks, and a details pane with the perk's rank, what is blocking it, each further point and the refund cost. Long trees scroll with the forward and back keys. The dash toggle sits under the archetype list.
	- Research is on the new frame: Overview, Body, Ammo, Weapons and Utility tabs. Each category is a list with icons showing what is researched, found or still unknown; pick an entry to see its picture, cost, discovery chance and description, with Research and Invest buttons beside it. Weapons split into Slot weapons and Luxury bundles. The left and right keys still step through the entries, and budget donation sits on the Overview.
	- The other menu pages keep their old look for now.
* New elite property "Curse Immune": the monster is unaffected by curses, unless a skill states it can affect curse immune enemies.
	- Can roll on Elite and Tough monsters from monster level 30.
* New very rare gem roles, for curse skills only:
	- Hexmaster: Socketed curse can affect curse immune enemies. 50% more mana cost. Greater: 33% more mana cost.
	- Ritualist: Socketed curse lingers in its area for 3 seconds, re-cursing every enemy inside it, so their curse stays refreshed while they remain. Its effect is reduced by 30%. Greater: 4 seconds, effect reduced by 20%.
* New rare gem roles for spells that strike an enemy directly:
	- Serpent: The hit chains to up to 3 more enemies, losing 10% of its damage at each chain. 20% more mana cost. Greater: up to 5 more, losing 15% each, never below 60%. 33% more mana cost.
	- Hydra: The hit forks to up to 4 more enemies behind the target, at 10% less damage. 20% more mana cost. Greater: up to 7 more. 33% more mana cost.
	- The projectile visibly travels from enemy to enemy. A chained projectile explodes only at its last enemy, at that chain's damage; a forking one explodes at the first enemy it hits.
	- Serpent and Hydra do not work together on one spell: the gem in the earlier socket wins.
	- "Chain Lightning" adds the chains to its own, and "Forked Lightning" adds the forks to its own. Hydra on "Chain Lightning" forks off its first target, and Serpent on "Forked Lightning" chains off its target, each with its own lightning.
	- Works on every spell that strikes an enemy directly: "Fireball", "Pyroblast", "Ice Bolt", "Fire Jet", "Searing Bond", "Threaded Volley", "Charged Bolt", "Lightning Spear", "Shrapnel", "Glacial Spike", "Freezing Pulse", "Venom Gyre", "Ice Spear", "Frost Shards" and "Finger of Death" (its hops are its own beam), and on the bolts "Winter Orb" and the golems throw, by those spells' own gems. Not on the "Winter Orb" itself.
	- A piercing projectile ("Shrapnel", "Glacial Spike", "Freezing Pulse", "Venom Gyre", "Frost Shards" at rank 10, "Ice Spear" once it has grown) chains by turning itself from enemy to enemy, piercing everything on the way at that chain's damage, then flies on. With Hydra it ends at the first enemy it hits, except "Venom Gyre": its forks are gyres of their own, thrown on from that enemy, each turning home when its own time runs out.
	- "Freezing Pulse" turns as a whole wave and "Venom Gyre" keeps its own clock: the time and distance they spend chaining comes out of their flight, so a long chain can end early.
	- A spell thrown by another (the "Ice Bolt"s of "Winter Orb", a golem's throw) chains or forks by its own spell's gems.
	- Bouncing spells chain or fork only at the end of their bouncing: "Charged Bolt" from rank 10 when its last bounce before it fades was off an enemy, "Lightning Spear" from its last strike through the enemy.
	- Several chains running at once (several bolts or rocks) no longer jump back onto an enemy they already hit.
* Gems now count only where they can do something, and an inactive gem adds no mana cost either:
	- Summoning spells take the gems that speak of minions (and Resolute on "Summon: Doomguard", for its landing stun), and the ailment gems ("Pyromancer", "Cryomancer", "Venomancer", "Electromancer", "Sanguinomancer"), "Merciless" and "Executioner", which roll on their minions' own hits, and "Destroyer", whose resist pierce their minions' hits take. Your own damage, critical strike, leech, charge, "Archmage" and "Assassin" gems no longer count on them.
	- "Summon: Ghast" no longer takes minion critical strike or hit gems, as its attack is your "Dark Wind" and crits and rolls as that spell does. "Dark Portal" no longer takes minion damage, critical strike or hit gems, as it deals no damage.
* Spells your minions cast ("Summon: Ghast"'s "Dark Wind", "Summon: Doomguard"'s "Finger of Death", the golems' and the demon's throws) now gain from minion damage: increased minion damage adds to that spell's increases, and the summon's more minion damage (its own, "Legion Mastery", "Commander") multiplies it.
* "Summon: Death Knight"'s skull ignite now gains from minion damage the same way.
	- Spells that deal no damage (curses, "Shock", "Mark Prey") take no damage, critical strike, leech or ailment gems. Projectile ones still take projectile gems.
	- Spells that deal only damage over time ("Blaze", "Immolation", "Righteous Fire", "Noxious Blast", "Corrosive Breath", "Plague", "Contagion", "Essence Theft", "Death and Decay") take no critical strike, more hit damage, leech, "Archmage", "Assassin", "Powerful", "Wizard", "Venomancer" or "Sanguinomancer" gems.
	- "Venomancer" no longer counts on poison spells, which poison on their own.
	- "Witch" counts only on curses with an effect to raise, and "Ritualist" only on curses with an area to linger in.
	- "Quicktongue" no longer counts on curses, "Mark Prey", "Carrion Crows", "Unleash Earth" or summons limited to one at a time, which a second cast only replaces.
* Poison from Earth spells ("Noxious Blast", "Corrosive Breath", "Venom Gyre", "Poison Nova", "Toxic Trap" and the others) is now damage from that spell: its penetration, "Merciless", "Executioner" and your other spell modifiers reach every tick.
	- Poison a spell's hit inflicts, from "Venomancer" or "Agony", is that spell's too.
* "Chilling Breath" and "Avalanche" now grow with area of effect: the breath reaches further, and the avalanche lands further out.
* Area of effect modifiers no longer apply to "Poison Nova".
* "Storm Call" now has a duration: the 1.6 seconds before the strike. Duration modifiers lengthen it.
* "Influencer" now also raises "Legion Mastery" and "Summon Mastery"'s minion health, "Summon Mastery"'s added summon duration, and "Agony"'s rank 5 poison and bleed chance and rank 10 reduced poison.
* "Summon Mastery"'s added duration is now raised by the summon's own duration gems.
* New uncommon gem roles for summoning spells:
	- Voracious: Minions gain +3% critical strike chance. 33% more mana cost. Greater: +5%, 50% more mana cost.
	- Alpha: Minions gain +50% critical strike multiplier. 25% more mana cost. Greater: +100%, 40% more mana cost.
	- They count for the minions of the spell they are socketed in, and a gem swapped while its minions are out counts at once.
* New very rare gem role "Archon": 1.5% of a spell's hit damage is leeched as mana. 20% more mana cost. Greater: 3%, 35% more mana cost.
	- Mana leech works like life leech: it fills a pool that holds up to 33% of your unreserved mana and pays out 1% of your unreserved mana at once, then every 0.7 seconds. Reaching full mana ends the leech.
	- The instant leech modifiers now read "of leech is instant" and pay that share of mana leech at once as well. The incursion one's downside is now "reduced leech recovery rate", and slows mana leech too.
	- "More damage while leeching" now also holds while leeching mana.
* The dungeon modifier "Players cannot lifesteal" is now "Players cannot leech", and blocks mana leech too.
* Pending leech now shows on the bars it fills: a faint, darker stretch of the bar marks how far the leech still to be paid will fill it, and the bar glides up into it as the leech pays out.
	- On the mana bar for mana leech, and on the health bar for life leech, or the energy shield bar when life leech fills your shield.
	- The life leech counter on the status bar is removed.
	- Life and mana flasks show the same: while one is drinking, the bar it fills marks how far the rest of the drink will take it. Flasks with instant recovery show none.
* New elite property "Silencer": hits from the monster's attacks and spells have a 33% chance to silence you for 4 seconds; its damage over time does not. A new silence restarts the timer.
	- While silenced you cannot cast spells. A spell being cast never comes out, channelled spells end as if you let go ("Annihilus" releases what it has charged so far) and toggled spells switch off. Auras and passives keep running.
	- Can roll on Elite and Tough monsters from monster level 40.
* New elite property "Mana Burner": hits from the monster's attacks and spells burn 10% of your current mana, and you take the burned mana as extra lightning damage, reduced by your elemental resistance. Its damage over time does not.
	- Can roll on Elite and Tough monsters from monster level 30.
* Attributes now reward spreading your points:
	- The damage an attribute gives to weapons, spells and minions counts in full up to your level, and a quarter past it.
	- Dexterity also grants spell haste, up to 25%. Intellect also grants increased spell cooldown recovery rate, up to 20%. Both grow fastest from the first points.
	- Each attribute also helps builds that do not use it for damage, again growing fastest from the first points:
		+ Strength grants increased ammo capacity, special ammo included, up to 40%.
		+ Dexterity grants a chance to shake off homing projectiles, up to 25%.
		+ Intellect grants reduced curse effect on you, up to 20%.
* Spells now scale with attributes the way weapons do, shown in each spell's tooltip as its attunement:
	- Fire spells scale with Intellect. "Immolation", "Righteous Fire", "Heat Shield", "Searing Bond" and "Volcano" scale with Strength and Intellect, "Infernal Strike" mostly with Strength.
	- Cold spells scale with Intellect. "Ice Bolt", "Freezing Pulse", "Glacial Spike", "Chilling Breath", "Creeping Frost", "Frost Shards", "Winter Orb" and "Ice Spear" scale with Dexterity and Intellect, "Ice Nova" with Strength and Intellect. "Gust of Frost", "Glacial Cascade", "Avalanche" and "Summon: Ice Golem" mostly with Strength.
	- Lightning spells scale with Dexterity and Intellect, "Thunder Clap" with Strength and Intellect.
	- Earth's rock spells scale mostly with Strength, its poison spells evenly with Dexterity and Intellect.
	- Black spells scale with Intellect. "Summon: Zombie", "Summon: Skeleton", "Summon: Slayer Knight" and "Summon: Doomguard" also with Strength, "Summon: Ghast" and "Carrion Crows" with Dexterity. "Plague", "Dark Wind", "Essence Theft", "Finger of Death", "Contagion", "Implosion" and "Nether Walk" take a little Dexterity, "Soulstorm" and "Death and Decay" a little Strength.
* Minion rework:
	- Minion damage now grows with your level the way monster damage does, and with the attunement of the spell that summoned it.
	- Intellect no longer adds minion damage or minion health directly. Raised monsters no longer gain damage from Intellect.
	- Minions roll their own critical strikes: 5% chance and 150% multiplier, raised only by minion modifiers. They no longer use your crit chance.
	- Minion hits now meet monster resistances, as yours do: resistances and immunities, the resistance monsters gain with level, weaknesses, and every curse or debuff that lowers resistances all apply. Minion resist pierce answers them; your own pierce does not.
	- Spells your minions cast ("Summon: Ghast"'s "Dark Wind", "Summon: Doomguard"'s "Finger of Death", the golems' and the demon's casts, and their "Serpent" and "Hydra" hops) are the minion's damage too: they pierce with minion resist pierce instead of yours. The cast spell's own "Destroyer" still adds its pierce.
	- Ignites, bleeds and poisons your minions cause ("Pyromancer", "Sanguinomancer" and "Venomancer" on a summon, the Death Knight's burning skulls) are the minion's damage too, and pierce with minion resist pierce and the summon's "Destroyer". An enemy has one burn and one bleed: whoever applied the stronger one, you or a minion, owns it and its pierce.
	- Summon tooltips show the minion's health and the damage of each of its attacks at your level and gear, and what the next rank brings, instead of base numbers.
	- Each rank of a summon now adds its full health at every level. Health gains under 100 per rank used to be lost to level scaling.
	- Minions that cast your spells repeat them when that spell has "Quicktongue" socketed, as your own cast would.
	- A cooldown recovery gem socketed in a summon spell now also speeds its minion's own abilities, alongside minion cooldown recovery rate.
* New minion modifiers on spell items, rarer than other spell modifiers:
	- Increased minion damage, increased minion cooldown recovery rate and increased minion health.
	- Crit chance for minions (very rare), increased crit chance for minions and additional crit multiplier for minions.
	- Minion corporeal resistances (every damage type except magic) and minion magic resistance. These can raise a minion's resistance up to 75%.
	- +1 to pet cap (very rare), from item level 36. Items from dungeon final bosses and Ultimatum can roll +2 instead.
	- Minion physical pierce, minion elemental pierce and minion magic pierce, as common as the other pierce modifiers and with a wider range: up to 32 physical or magic and 27 elemental on a medium charm, from item level 20 (38 and 32 on items from dungeon final bosses and Ultimatum).
* The pet cap can no longer go above 10, whatever raises it.
* New Magi minion perk "Sharpened Claws" (3 points, needs 9 Magi points and "Devoted Leader" or "Inspirational"): minions gain 8% resist pierce on all damage types, and 4% more per additional point.
* New incursion modifiers that reward a second attribute, rarer than the other incursion modifiers and dropped only by incursion monsters:
	- Increased weapon damage per 10 Intellect, up to 11% on armor, helms and boots.
	- Increased spell damage per 10 Strength, and the same per 10 Dexterity, up to 8% on armor, helms and boots.
	- As with attribute damage, points past your level count a quarter.
* New "Skill Wheel" for your spell hotbar, under Controls in "DnD Spells":
	- Hold the key, move the mouse towards a spell and let go to cast it. Letting go in the middle casts nothing.
	- You keep moving while it is open. Your view stays put, so the spell goes where you were looking when you opened it.
	- Spells on cooldown show grey, with the time left under the spell's name.
	- The borders of the slot under the cursor light up.

==== BUG FIXES / TWEAKS ====
* Spell tree lines redrawn so they no longer suggest requirements that do not exist:
	- Spells that share the same requirements share one set of lines, and no other line ever joins it. Two unrelated lines that cross pass over each other instead of meeting.
	- Hovering a spell highlights the lines of everything it requires and everything it opens.
	- A line now turns gold once its requirement is met. This was meant to happen before but never showed.
	- The Cold tree is rearranged so its lines no longer tangle. No spell's requirements changed.
* Monster resistance from level reworked:
	- It now rises smoothly from level 36 and stops growing at level 83, where it reaches 40% to every damage type. It used to jump to 30% at level 41 and keep climbing in steps, to 78% at level 100.
	- Monsters have more health in its place, so a hit with no resist pierce kills them about as fast as before, without the sudden jump at level 41. Resist pierce is worth having rather than required.
	- Past level 83 monsters keep getting tougher through health alone.
	- A Nuclear elite's explosion and Doomguy's demon soul heal are unchanged by the extra health.
* Monsters with very large health (big custom bosses) no longer break fortify, healing, chill, Incinerate and other effects that work from their maximum health.
* Item drops from kills reworked:
	- Every monster now has a loot value from its type, rarity, Tough or Elite status and modifiers, and a map shares its loot out by that value. A lone Baron on a small map is an event; on a map packed with Barons each one is worth much less. Bigger and harder maps still pay more in total, just not in proportion.
	- Overall, maps of up to a few hundred weaker monsters drop about 60% more items, dense maps of strong monsters about 30% more, and slaughter maps about 5-10% more.
	- Monster level no longer changes how many items drop. It still raises their item level and orb stacks.
	- An Elite's drops are always well rolled.
	- The Stats page shows how much a kill pays on this map compared with an average map.
	- Loot chests, research and spell points from kills still scale with how many monsters a map has, more per kill on smaller maps. Your drop chance, item quantity, unique item odds, chest contents, soul and ammo drops do not change with map size.
* Increased drop chance now always counts for item drops from kills: +100% drop chance means twice the item drops. Before, the first +50% did nothing once your starting luck ran out, and at that drop chance stronger monsters dropped items no more often than weaker ones.
* New highlight drops:
	- Every kill fills a hidden meter of your own, saved with your character, by the monster's loot value. Monsters far below your level fill it much more slowly.
	- Any kill can give a highlight, more likely as the meter fills and certain once it is full. Each highlight makes the next harder for a while, easing off as you keep playing.
	- A highlight is an extra item of any equipment type: well rolled, one more mod where it has room, twice the unique chance, two item levels higher within its usual cap, and a chance at synergy like a gold chest's. It drops in a large coloured bag with its own sound; the colour shows how far it synergised.
	- Ordinary drops from kills no longer roll synergy, so a coloured bag from a kill is always a highlight. Chests, the merchant and Ultimatum keep theirs.

* The mana bar and monster health bars no longer break into blocky patches on Vulkan renderers the longer a game runs. Their flowing patterns are laid out a little differently.
* Elite and Tough monster properties now respect their level requirements. Below monster level 50, monsters could roll properties meant for higher levels, and some low level properties never appeared.
* "Fire Immune", "Ice Immune", "Poison Immune" and "Lightning Immune" elite properties now actually make the monster immune to that element.
	- They count towards the limit of 2 immunities per monster, and "dnd_no_immunity" disables them like the other immunities.
* "dnd_no_immunity" and "dnd_no_reflect" now work together. With both on, immunities could still roll.
* Help menu: added "Warding Aura" to the defensive monster properties, and the special monster properties page shows the right descriptions again.
* Regenerating monsters pause their regeneration while at full health, and resume within a second of being hurt.
* The "Ruby Wand" fire trail and the "Thunder Staff" lightning zaps show again on maps that have their own scripts. They never appeared there.
* "Earth Lich" no longer runs out of spike volleys. It could cast only 30 spikes per life and then threw nothing but rocks.
* "Onimuz the Unbroken" no longer stands still forever after casting its projection. It now stops channeling once the projection expires, or when the projection never forms.
* "Blood Magic": auras can no longer reserve your last point of life. They used to run for free at 1 max life.
	- At 1 max life ("Protoshell", or "Sanguine Covenant" without energy shield) auras switch off and cannot be switched on.
	- Auras reserving 100% of your life together no longer fit, the last one switches off.
* Performance: firing weapons, monster spawning on large maps and starting a new game cost much less script time, on the server and on clients.
* Performance: loading a character in Softcore and Hardcore reads the database much faster, so the server no longer stutters while a player loads.
* Performance: effects that look for monsters around them now look only nearby instead of checking every monster on the map: melee splash, poison and ignite spreading, overload zaps, "Thumper", "Tailwind", "Flash Parry", "Crash Course", and the "Thunder Staff", "Gravdis 5000", "Sickle", "Dark Lance" and chain lightning weapons. Large maps no longer slow down when they trigger.
* Performance: kills and raises no longer count every monster on the map to check your minion limit, a level-up no longer rebuilds every weapon's damage at once, and the HUD costs less script time every tic.
* Melee splash, "Thumper", "Tailwind", "Flash Parry" and "Crash Course" now measure their reach as a true distance, height included: they no longer reach monsters far above or below you, and reach their full distance diagonally too.
* "Faraday Halo": the arc now reaches the overloaded enemies in its sight within twice the overload zap's range, instead of every overloaded enemy on the map.
* "Thumper", "Flash Parry" and "Crash Course" now deal their damage. It never landed before. They are physical attacks: physical attack damage, increased physical attack damage, attribute bonuses and physical penetration all apply, and "Thumper" and "Flash Parry" also gain from increased attack radius damage.
* "Flash Parry" now hits the enemies in front of you. Its cone never matched anything before.
* Poison spreading on kill now reaches further with area of effect, as ignite proliferation does.
* Performance: spells that look for enemies around them (targeting, curses, chains and forks, novas, auras and ground areas) now look only nearby instead of checking every monster on the map.
* Performance: several hits on one monster in the same tic (shotgun pellets, splash) work out its resistances once. A debuff landed during that tic counts from the next one.
* "Ice Nova" now spreads as a sphere: enemies far above or below you are no longer hit, and those up close in any direction are.
* "Forked Lightning" and the "Hydra" gem fork to enemies within their reach in every direction, height included, instead of any height.
* "Gust of Frost" no longer reaches enemies more than 128 units above or below it.
* "Storm Brand", "Monsoon", "Thunder Clap", "Storm Call", "Baleful Infusion"'s spread, "Vines of Torment" and "Upheaval" no longer reach enemies far above or below their area.
* Martialist "Expose Weakness": the parry that applies it no longer deals its hit as the wrong damage type.
* "Gravdis 5000": the pull after its blast now draws in only the monsters near that blast, instead of every monster held by any Gravdis on the map.
* "Thunder Staff": the alt-fire's lightning now strikes only the monsters its ring reached. Enemies left marked by an earlier ring, or another player's, are no longer struck.
* "GraveCaller": raised zombies are now the zombie "Summon: Zombie" conjures, with its health, melee damage and duration at your "Summon: Zombie" rank, or rank 1 if you have not learned it.
	- The charm keeps raising while you have fewer than 5 zombies, counting the ones you summon yourself.
* Fixed effects attached to the first monsters of a map (elite sparkles, reflect shields and similar) clashing with "Ultimatum" objects. An Ultimatum could remove a monster's effect, or wait on a popped pustule that never counted as gone.
* "Demon Sealer": Kanji Trap explosions from players other than the first no longer clash with other effects, such as parries.
* Pet icons no longer stay over dead pets.
* Monster spells now count as magic damage against your armor, and armor piercing monsters pierce your armor only with their attacks.
* Help menu: "Blackout", the poison, chill, ignite and overload properties and ammo theft now say they work with the monster's spells too, as they always have.
* Fixed pets writing their icon data into memory used by other systems, which could cause hard to trace problems while pets were out.
* Fixed the pet limit being ignored in multiplayer after dying: pets that outlived you no longer counted towards it, so every death let you summon more.
	- Pets now die when their summoner dies or leaves the game.
	- Summoning at a full limit no longer sends home two pets to make room for one.
	- A summon that cannot make room, such as when raised minions fill your limit, is no longer cast, and costs nothing.
* Pets without a time limit ("Summon: Rock Golem", "Summon: Ice Golem", "Summon: Fire Demon", and any minion a mastery takes the time limit off) now follow you to the next map when the level is exited, up to 10 of them, at the health they had, with no mana, cooldown or cast time.
	- They are also saved with your character and return when you load it. Pets that died with you are not saved.
* Skill and gem pockets: long headings, such as "In: Summon: Ice Golem", shrink to fit instead of running past the frame.
* A new character no longer shows the flasks of your previous character on the HUD. They could not be drunk. The same applied after losing a hardcore character.
* Attribute descriptions show the right numbers: Strength gives 1.5 maximum life and 0.5 stamina per point, Intellect 0.5 energy shield and 1.5 mana.
* "Rally" reaches further with its own level instead of with Intellect: 128 units at level 1, up to 344 at level 10.
* "Annihilus" damage per rank increased from 25 to 30.
* "Volcano" lava damage increased from 60 to 80, and now grows by 20 per rank (it did not grow before).
	- Lava bounces off ceilings instead of bursting on them, so low rooms no longer cut its throws short.
	- Lava is thrown at a much wider range of distances, and some of it always lands close to the volcano.
	- Releasing it early now shortens its cooldown: it recovers 8% faster for each charge not built, up to 32%. Releasing after 1 second recovers 24% faster.
	- The volcano is now a 3D model with lava flowing down its sides. It rises out of the ground, each eruption bursts out of the crater with flames, embers and a plume of ash, and it sinks back into the ground when it ends.
* Fixed "Searing Bond" below rank 10 also exploding around what it hit, for its full damage over 100 units. Only rank 10 explodes.
* "Fire Jet" is now drawn as a 3D flame: a white-hot head whose fire splits into tongues streaming out behind it, shedding embers as it flies.
	- The side flames are now small 3D flames like the jet, diving to the floor and flaring up where they land.
	- Each side flame now bursts where it lands, damaging enemies within 48 units of it, so the flames no longer have to touch an enemy to hurt it. Below rank 10 a side flame only damages through this burst.
	- The side flames now gain the same "Blaze", "Fireball" and "Pyroblast" synergies as the direct hit.
	- Rank 10's ripping side flames now hit each enemy they pass through once, instead of again on every tic they are inside it.
	- Rank 5's explosion now deals full damage instead of half.
* The cast bar and the silence bar no longer glow a few pixels past the front of the fill along their top and bottom edges.
* Items with percent Strength, or that turn Strength's effects off, now update stamina, knockback resistance and ammo capacity as soon as they are equipped or removed. Items granting all attributes, percent attributes or "Unity" now update stamina too.
* Losing Strength from an item or an attribute refund now lowers your life to the new maximum, and healing respects a Strength item's new maximum right away instead of after a map change.
* Weapon Wheel: the dotted borders between weapons are now solid lines coming out from under the middle ring, and the borders of the weapon under the cursor light up.
* Weapon Wheel now opens while its key is held and switches to the weapon under the cursor when you let go. Your view stays put while it is open and you keep moving. Rebind it under Controls, "DnD Keys".
* While choosing your class, the DnD menu, inventory, stash, weapon and skill wheels, current weapon tip and NPC conversations no longer open over the class screen.
* The software renderer is no longer supported: the mana bar, cast bar, monster health bar and wheels only draw their OpenGL look.
* Minion modifiers (minion damage, health, cooldown recovery, critical strike chance and multiplier, resistances) now show on the Utility stats page.
* Modifiers with 6 to 8 tiers no longer fall as far below the tier their item level offers when a roll comes out low. The chance of rolling the best tiers is unchanged.
	- Example: a 6 tier modifier on a level 83 item used to roll its lowest tier 16% of the time; its lowest possible roll is now 2 tiers higher.
* Monsters on a map played after a dungeon could still count as that dungeon's boss, which made them harder to knock back and let effects that skip dungeon bosses skip them too. Fixed.
* Fixed one player being able to spend another player's credits at the merchant, or act on another player's character slots, by sending menu requests in their name.
* Trading:
	- Confirming again after a trade failed for lack of space could destroy an item in the receiving inventory. Space is now checked again when the countdown ends, and nothing moves if it no longer fits.
	- A trade that failed for lack of space could leave an inventory cell that refused pickups while it looked empty.
	- Orb stacks can no longer go over their limit through a trade, whether from several offers onto one stack or from both players offering the same orb.
	- Leaving the game in the middle of a trade no longer loses the items you had offered.
