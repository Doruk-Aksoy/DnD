COLD
------
 
* Ice Bolt
 
	Send a bolt of ice forward to deal 50 damage. Slows enemies by 30% on impact.
	Cost: 5 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: Damage increases by 25, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Slow becomes 75%.
	Rank 10: Affects 96 unit radius area around impact.
	Unlocks: Glacial Spike, Chilling Breath.
	Synergy - Glacial Spike: 2.5% damage per rank of Glacial Spike.
	Synergy - Frost Shards: 2.5% damage per rank of Frost Shards.
	Synergy - Orb of Winter: 5% damage per rank of Orb of Winter.
	
* Freezing Pulse

	Cast a wave of cold hugging the floor, ripping through enemies once for 30 damage, with limited range. Has 20% base chance to freeze enemies, diminishing to 0%
	as it travels.
	Cost: 6 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +15 damage, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Goes 25% further.
	Rank 10: Width increases by 25%.
	Unlocks: Creeping Frost.
	Synergy - Creeping Frost: 5% more damage per rank of Creeping Frost.
	Synergy - Gust of Frost: 5% more range per rank of Gust of Frost. 
 
* Glacial Spike
 
	Sends forth a sharp spike of ice that rips through at most 10 enemies, dealing 25 damage. On impact, shatters to freeze nearby enemies for 1 second.
	Cost: 12 mana.
	Cooldown: 2 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.7 seconds.
	Per Rank: Damage increases by 5, +1.8 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Freeze duration becomes 2 seconds.
	Rank 10: Can rip through enemies with "Hardened Skin".
	Requires: Ice Bolt (Rank 3). Player level 8.
	Synergy - Ice Bolt: 5% damage per rank of Ice Bolt.
	Synergy - Freezing Pulse: +1 rip count.
 
* Chilling Breath
 
	Breathe winds of cold to deal 30 damage every 0.5 seconds in a cone in front of you. Channeled.
	Cost: 4 mana every 0.5 seconds.
	Cooldown: 2 seconds.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Damage increases by 6, +0.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Slows enemies by 50%.
	Rank 10: Cone becomes 30% wider.
	Requires: Ice Bolt (Rank 3). Player level 8.
	Synergy: Ice Bolt: 3% damage per rank.
	
* Creeping Frost

	Fire an icey projectile that explodes upon impact and creeps along the floor. It deals 30 damage on impact and deals 100 damage per second as it crawls
	towards nearby targets. Lasts 5 seconds. Cannot stagger enemies.
	Cost: 10 mana.
	Cooldown: 2.5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: 10 impact damage and 20 damage over time, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Lasts 2 seconds longer.
	Rank 10: Can crawl 64 units higher.
	Requires: Freezing Pulse (Rank 2). Player level 9.
	Synergy - Gust of Frost: 3% faster creeping speed per rank.
	Synergy - Freezing Pulse: 2.5% more damage over time per rank.
 
* Ice Shield:
 
	Creates 8 ice barriers around you that constantly rotate and block enemy attacks. 75 health per barrier, 5 second duration.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 18 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 health, +0.5 sec duration, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: +2.5 second duration.
	Rank 10: Blocks ripper type attacks.
	Requires: Ice Bolt (Rank 2). Player level 12.
 
* Frost Shards
 
	Create 5 frost shards every 0.5 seconds in front of you over 1.5 seconds that deal 16 damage each.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +5 damage per shard, +1.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: 2 more shards launched every 0.5 seconds.
	Rank 10: Shards rip through enemies.
	Requires: Glacial Spike (Rank 2). Player level 16.
	Synergy - Glacial Spike: 2.5% more damage per shard per rank of Glacial Spike.
	Synergy - Ice Bolt: 2.5% more damage per shard per rank of Ice Bolt.
 
* Gust of Frost
 
	Unleash a chilling wind that pushes enemies away and slows them by 30%.
	Cost: 18 mana.
	Cooldown: 12 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: -1 cooldown, +1.2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Slow becomes 60%.
	Rank 10: At the end of it's distance, tosses enemies aside. Enemies hitting walls take damage proportional to their mass.
	Requires: Chilling Breath (Rank 2). Player level 16.
	
* Frost Bomb

	Create a pulsing ice crystal at the targeted location, dealing 60 damage per pulse in a 128 unit radius every 0.5 seconds.
	The crystal lasts for 5 seconds, and applies a debuff that prevents enemies from regenerating life for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 15 mana.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +5 damage per pulse, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Applies cold exposure on pulses, reducing enemy cold resistance by 15%.
	Rank 10: Radius increases to 180.
	Requires: Chilling Breath (Rank 2). Player level 18.
	
* Ice Nova

	Conjure a nova of ice emanating from you, doing 200 damage in an expanding ring up to 320 units.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 1 second.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +16 unit radius and 20 damage, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Ring expands 20% faster.
	Rank 10: Enemies hit near the furthest section are debuffed for 3 seconds, dealing 15% less damage.
	Requires: Ice Shield (Rank 1). Player level 14.
	Synergy - Ice Shield: +8 radius per rank.
 
* Summon: Ice Golem
 
	Summons a golem made of ice to fight along your side.
	Has 500 (+10/INT) health and a melee attack that deals 30 (+5/INT) damage.
	Cost: 25 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: Health +50, damage +5, +2.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Melee attacks have 25% chance to freeze the enemy for 1 second.
	Rank 10: Can cast Ice Bolt with same level as you.
	Requires: Ice Shield (Rank 3). Player level 20.
	Synergy - Ice Shield: 2% more health per rank of Ice Shield.
	Synergy - Gust of Frost: 2% more speed per rank of Gust of Frost.
 
* Hatred
 
	PASSIVE SKILL - AURA SKILL
	Generate an aura that increases you and nearby allies' cold damage by 15%. Radius is 256 units.
	Cost: 30% mana reserved.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Damage increases by 1.5%.
	Rank 5: Radius is doubled.
	Rank 10: Aura gives 10% additional crit chance to cold attacks.
	Requires: Ice Shield (Rank 5). Player level 24.
	
* Frostbite
	
	CURSE
	Curses enemies in a targeted area around 256 units, causing them to have -30% to cold resistance for 8 seconds. They also gain 25% base chance to be chilled.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: None.
	Per Rank: -1 additional cold resistance reduction, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: 25% increased area of effect.
	Rank 10: +4 seconds to duration.
	Requires: Frost Shards (Rank 1) or Frost Bomb (Rank 1). Player level 30.
 
* Blizzard
 
	Call the winds of the north to form a storm, raining ice in a location every second for 5 seconds.
	Ice deals 90 damage in a 64 unit radius. Blizzard has effect in 224 unit radius.
	Cost: 60 mana.
	Cooldown: 18 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +15 damage per ice, +4 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Radius +96.
	Rank 10: Rate of ice falling increases by 30%.
	Requires: Frost Shards (Rank 3), Gust of Frost (Rank 2). Player level 32.
	Synergy - Frost Shards: 5% more damage per rank of Frost Shards.
	Synergy - Glacial Spike: 2.5% more damage per rank of Glacial Spike.
	Synergy - Ice Bolt: +12 radius area of effect on main area per rank of Ice Bolt.
 
* Winter Orb
 
	Release a slow moving orb that slows enemies by 30% for 0.75 seconds and throwing Ice Bolts as it travels in a spiral pattern.
	Orb deals 100 damage as it travels. Ice Bolts scale from your Ice Bolt rank.
	Cost: 80 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: Orb damage +50, +4 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Orb freezes enemies it goes through instead for 0.75 seconds.
	Rank 10: Orb also throws Frost Shards.
	Requires: Gust of Frost (Rank 1), Summon: Ice Golem (Rank 2). Player level 36.
	Synergy - Ice Bolt: 5% more damage for the Orb per rank of Ice Bolt.
	Synergy - Frost Shards: 2.5% more damage for the Orb per rank of Frost Shards.
	Synergy - Gust of Frost: Freeze duration increases by 0.2 per rank of Gust of Frost.
	
* Ice Spear

	Hurl a spear made of pure ice, travelling a short distance, dealing 50 damage. Afterwards, it evolves into a stronger form, travelling much faster and gaining 
	additional critical strike chance and multiplier, as well as ripping through 10 enemies for 250 damage. Gains 10% added critical strike chance and 50% crit multi.
	Cost: 35 mana.
	Cooldown: 6 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +5 initial damage, +25 second phase damage, +2.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: +2 added critical strike chance.
	Rank 10: +50% added critical strike multiplier.
	Requires: Ice Nova (Rank 2) or Summon: Ice Golem (Rank 2). Player level 24.
	Synergy - Ice Bolt: 5% more damage per rank.
	Synergy - Ice Nova: 2.5% more travel speed.
	
* Glacial Cascade

	An advancing wave of ice crashes forwards in a series of four bursts, each doing 20% more damage than last. The final burst does 100% more damage.
	Initial burst starts with 200 damage. Knocks enemies back once.
	Cost: 45 mana.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +50 damage, +3.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Range increased by 25%.
	Rank 10: Width increases each burst by 20%.
	Requires: Ice Spear (Rank 2). Player level 44.
	Synergy - Ice Spear: 5% more damage per rank.
	Synergy - Glacial Spike: 2.5% more range per rank.
 
* Shivering Armor
 
	Enchant your or a target ally's armor to reduce damage taken by 15% for 5 seconds.
	Enemies that hit the enchanted armor have 20% chance to be frozen for 1 second.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 18 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: Damage reduction +1.5%, +2.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: If you target an ally, grants effects to you. If you target nobody, grants effect to nearest ally.
	Rank 10: Armor stops ripping attacks.
	Requires: Hatred (Rank 1). Player level 46.
	Synergy - Ice Shield: Armor points granted +25 per rank of Ice Shield.
 
* Avalanche
 
	After a brief delay, casts down enormous amounts of ice behind you towards your direction.
	Ice deals 16 damage. Total amount of ice thrown is 75.
	Cost: 120 mana.
	Cooldown: 45 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Ice damage +3, +2 more ice thrown, +18 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Ice thrown have 15% chance to freeze enemies for 1 second.
	Rank 10: Ice gains 25% chance to rip through enemies.
	Requires: Blizzard (Rank 1), Winter Orb (Rank 1). Player level 52.
	Synergy - Blizzard: 2.5% more damage per rank of Blizzard.
	Synergy - Chilling Breath: 2.5% more damage per rank of Chilling Breath.
	Synergy - Gust of Frost: 2.5% more damage per rank of Gust of Frost.
