EARTH
-----

* Shrapnel
 
	Unearth a shard of rock from the ground and throw it to penetrate the enemies.
	Deals 60 physical damage. Has a maximum rip count of 10.
	Cost: 6 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.85 seconds.
	Per Rank: +15 damage. At every even rank gains an extra rip count.
	Rank 5: Rips through all enemies regardless of "Hardened Skin" modifier.
	Rank 10: Shoots three shards instead of one.
	Unlocks: Rockslide.
	Synergy - Rockslide: 5% more damage per Rockslide rank.
 
* Noxious Blast
 
	Target a location within 512 units to create a poisonous explosion after a short delay in a 96 unit radius. Enemies caught are poisoned for 3 seconds.
	Deals 50 damage per second.
	Cost: 8 mana.
	Cooldown: 2 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.75 seconds.
	Per Rank: +15 damage.
	Rank 5: Poison duration +1 second.
	Rank 10: Radius increases to 160 units.
	Unlocks: Toxic Trap, Poison Nova.
	Synergy - Poison Nova: 5% damage per rank of Poison Nova.
	Synergy - Toxic Trap: 5% damage per rank of Toxic Trap.
 
* Rockslide
 
	Form a massive boulder of rock from the ground and toss it forward with an arc.
	Enemies hit are stunned for 1.5 seconds and take 250 damage in a 96 unit radius with fall off radius of 48.
	Cost: 15 mana.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +75 damage, -0.5 second cooldown.
	Rank 5: Boulder speed increases by 50%.
	Rank 10: Radius increases to 180, stun duration to 2.5 seconds.
	Unlocks: Threaded Volley, Upheaval.
	Requires: Shrapnel (3 ranks), Player Level 15.
	Synergy - Shrapnel: 5% damage per rank of Shrapnel.
	Synergy - Rock Armor: +8 to radius per rank of Rock Armor.
	
* Blockade

	Arise a slanted wall of rock from the ground up to 384 units in front of you to block any enemy or their attacks.
	The wall is 96 units long and can withstand up to 250 damage.
	Cost: 18 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +16 wall length, +75 wall health.
	Rank 5: You and your allies can shoot through the wall.
	Rank 10: When the wall breaks, sends a volley of 8 Shrapnels lined up next to each other.
	Unlocks: Rock Armor (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Rock Armor: 5% increased wall health.
	Synergy - Summon: Rock Golem: +8 wall length per rank.
	
* Baleful Infusion

	Enchant your next melee attack to convert 50% of the damage it'd deal into becoming poison instead with a guaranteed poison infliction.
	Buff lasts for 10 seconds or until you expend the attack.
	Cost: 16 mana.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: 5% damage conversion.
	Rank 5: Enchanted attack pierces 20 poison resistance.
	Rank 10: Enemies killed within 2 seconds of being hit inflict enemies within 192 unit radius with the same poison.
	Requires: Noxious Blast (Rank 3), Player Level 14.
	Synergy - Poison Nova: For every 2 ranks the poison lasts 1 additional second inflicted from this attack.
 
* Toxic Trap
 
	Lay a proximity trap that activates when a monster comes into contact. Releases toxic gas clouds around.
	Gas clouds do 32 damage every 0.25 seconds in 72 unit radius and last 5 seconds.
	Traps last 30 seconds. Can have a maximum of 2 traps active.
	Cost: 18 mana.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +8 damage.
	Rank 5: +2 max traps active.
	Rank 10: Gas clouds last an extra second and the traps last an additional 15 seconds.
	Unlocks: Vines of Torment, Corrosive Breath.
	Requires: Noxious Blast (Rank 3). Player Level 15.
	Synergy - Noxious Blast: 5% damage per rank of Noxious Blast.
	Synergy - Poison Nova: Radius of gas clouds +8 per rank of Poison Nova.
	
* Venom Gyre

	Send a bouncing toxic projectile ripping through enemies once to deal 150 damage like a boomerang.
	Has a 50% chance to inflict poison dealing 300 damage over 3 seconds.
	Has a rip count of 5. The projectile will fire back at you after 5 seconds or expiry of rips, which can then be caught.
	If caught normally, grants a buff increasing poison damage dealt by 10% for 5 seconds.
	If caught during a dash, it sends a fresh Venom Gyre that deals double damage after 0.3 seconds but it only goes straight and cannot bounce.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.7 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 impact damage, +60 damage over time.
	Rank 5: Rip count increases to 10.
	Rank 10: Fires 2 additional Venom Gyres.
	Unlocks: 
	Requires: Baleful Infusion (Rank 2). Player level 24.
 
* Rock Armor
 
	Build an extra layer of armor made of rocks protect you from attacks.
	Completely absorbs all damage except magical which it only has 50% effectiveness against. Grants 80 damage absorbing points. Lasts for 8 seconds.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +40 armor points.
	Rank 5: Lasts for 2 more seconds.
	Rank 10: When the armor is shattered, launchees 8 Shrapnels around you in a circle.
	Unlocks: Summon: Rock Golem.
	Requires: Blockade (Rank 2). Player Level 24.
	Synergy - Summon: Rock Golem: +1 to duration per rank of Summon: Rock Golem.
	Synergy - Blockade: 5% more points per rank of Blockade.
 
* Upheaval
 
	Create a tectonic rift, cracking the surface in multiple ways in front of you up to 480 units far and 256 units wide.
	The rifts shake and explode initially after 1.5 seconds for 100 physical damage.
	This initial explosion tosses enemies up in the air, stunning them for 2 seconds.
	The ground becomes unstable and explodes after 2 seconds from this initial explosion, dealing 300 physical damage.
	Initial throw deals 100 plus up to an additional 500 physical damage based on their mass.
	Cost: 45 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +75 throw damage, +25 initial explosion damage, +125 subsequent explosion damage. +16 width and distance.
	Rank 5: Thrown enemies are stunned for 3 seconds instead.
	Rank 10: The unstable explosion damage is repeats 2 additional times in 1 second intervals.
	Unlocks: Crushing Rocks.
	Requires: Rockslide (Rank 3). Player Level 24.
	Synergy - Rockslide: 2.5% more explosion damage per rank of Rockslide.
	Synergy - Shrapnel: 2.5% more toss damage per rank of Shrapnel.
	Synergy - Crushing Rocks: 2.5% more damage per rank of Crushing Rocks.
	
* Threaded Volley

	Draw energies from the ground and form shards of 5 rocks, launching them over 3 seconds in the direction you're facing.
	They deal 80 physical damage on impact and 40 physical damage in an 80 unit radius.
	A 256 unit radius area around where you cast becomes energized, subsequent casts within this area instead send a single rock
	dealing 450 damage in a 160 unit radius. It explodes into small chunks of rocks that rip through enemies for 16 damage with max rip count of 4.
	Energized areas last for 10 seconds. Casts of this spell within energized areas have 75% spell haste and 30% less cost.
	Cost: 36 mana.
	Cooldown: 12 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +25 small rock impact damage, +15 small rock area damage. +175 energized rock damage.
	Rank 5: Casting in overlaps of energized areas grant 20% more damage to this spell per overlap.
	Rank 10: Energized areas instead cover 384 unit radius.
	Unlocks: Vulnerability.
	Requires: Rockslide (Rank 2) or Blockade (Rank 2). Player level 28.
	Synergy - Rockslide: 2.5% more damage per rank to the energized rock.
	Synergy - Shrapnel: 2.5% more damage per rank to regular rocks.
 
* Vines of Torment
 
	Create a whiplash of vines travelling forward coming up from the ground to snare enemies caught in them.
	Deals 100 damage and snares enemies hit by 3 seconds. Vines last up to 2.5 seconds.
	Cost: 35 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 damage. At every even rank, snare lasts +0.5 seconds.
	Rank 5: Vine width increases by 25%.
	Rank 10: Vines travel 50% farther.
	Unlocks: Geyser.
	Requires: Toxic Trap (Rank 1). Player Level 22.
	Synergy - Toxic Trap: +3% vine speed.
	
* Corrosive Breath

	Build up corrosion and a deadly stench then release it over 1.5 seconds in front of you in a 60 degree cone as toxic fumes.
	Fumes inflict poison dealing 600 damage over 5 seconds. Afflicted enemies are slowed by 25% and are 10% more likely to be poisoned by subsequent damage for 3 seconds.
	Afflicted fortified enemies have their fortification decay during this time, losing up to 50% of it.
	Cost: 32 mana.
	Cooldown: 18 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +100 poison damage.
	Rank 5: Enemies are instead 25% more likely to be poisoned by subsequent attacks.
	Rank 10: Cone angle increases to 90 and there are 33% more fumes during the release.
	Unlocks: Poison Nova.
	Requires: Toxic Trap (Rank 3). Player Level 34.
	Synergy - Toxic Trap: -0.5 cooldown per rank.
	Synergy - Noxious Blast: 2.5% more damage per rank.
 
* Agony
 
	PASSIVE SKILL - AURA SKILL
	Casts an aura that grants all nearby allies and yourself to grant more damage over time by 18% in a 256 unit radius.
	Cost: 30% mana reserved.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Radius +16, +1% damage over time.
	Rank 5: Enemies within aura radius gain a base 10% chance to be poisoned and bleed.
	Rank 10: Poison duration on you and allies within radius is reduced by 25%.
	Unlocks: Geyser.
	Requires: Venom Gyre (Rank 2) or Vines of Torment (Rank 2). Player Level 24.
 
* Summon: Rock Golem
 
	Summons a very durable rock golem to stand and fight for you.
	Golem has 750 health and does 100 melee damage.
	Cost: 75 mana.
	Cooldown: 45 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +250 health, +25 damage.
	Rank 5: Golem gains ability to cast Rockslide.
	Rank 10: Golem becomes immune to elemental attacks and gains explosive resist.
	Requires: Rock Armor (Rank 1). Player Level 35.
	Synergy - Rock Armor: +5% more health per rank of Rock Armor.
	
* Vulnerability

	CURSE
	Curses enemies in a targeted area around 256 units, causing them to have -30% to physical resistance for 8 seconds. They also gain 25% base chance to bleed.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: None.
	Per Rank: -1 additional physical resistance reduction, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: 25% increased area of effect.
	Rank 10: +4 seconds to duration.
	Requires: Upheaval or Threaded Volley. Player level 30.
 
* Crushing Rocks
 
	Summon two huge pieces of rock to the sides of a target enemy. The rocks will then attempt to crush the enemy.
	Rocks do 270 physical damage for each crushing attempt. Total of 5 attempts.
	Cost: 80 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +90 damage.
	Rank 5: Damage affects 96 unit radius area instead.
	Rank 10: Crush attempts increase to 7.
	Unlocks: Geyser.
	Requires: Upheaval (Rank 1). Player Level 36.
	Synergy - Rockslide: 5% more damage per rank of Rockslide.
	
* Despair

	CURSE
	Curses enemies in a targeted area around 256 units, causing them to have -30% to poison resistance for 10 seconds. They also gain 25% base chance to be poisoned.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: None.
	Per Rank: -1 additional poison resistance reduction, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: 25% increased area of effect.
	Rank 10: Cursed enemies cannot regenerate health.
	Requires: Vines of Torment or Corrosive Breath. Player level 30.
	
* Poison Nova
 
	Create a nova of poison around you that deals damage over time as they pass through enemies. Creates 6 projectiles.
	Each projectile deals 50 damage directly and 900 damage over 4 seconds. Projectiles last 4 seconds.
	Cost: 35 mana.
	Cooldown: 10 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 direct hit damage, +150 damage over time.
	Rank 5: +4 more projectiles created.
	Rank 10: Projectiles last 50% longer.
	Requires: Corrosive Breath (Rank 2). Player Level 36.
	Synergy - Noxious Blast: 2.5% damage per rank of Noxious Blast.
	Synergy - Corrosive Breath: Poison lasts an additional 0.2 seconds per rank.
	
* Unleash Earth
	
	Begin concentrating after 0.33 seconds of casting. You can channel to create pillars to erupt underneath where you target up to 640 units away.
	The pillars erupt after 0.2 seconds, rise up to anywhere between 128 to 384 units or just shy of the ceiling to then deal 800 physical damage
	and toss any enemy under them upwards and slightly sideways depending on the orientation of the pillar. Flung enemies crash land to take up to 
	1200 physical damage based on their mass and how high they were sent flying. Enemies can be repeatedly flung if they get caught multiple times during channeling 
	to increase the subsequent damage they take for 25%. Each cast of a single pillar requires 0.33 seconds of break until you can cast again.
	Bosses and Dungeon Bosses are sent flying for a very limited time and speed.
	Pillars have 96 unit radius.
	Cost: 80 mana per cast of a pillar.
	Cooldown: 100 seconds after channel ends.
	Cast Time: 0.33 seconds. (This is irreducible)
	Per Rank: +400 physical damage on pillar hits and cap of toss damage.
	Rank 5: Can cast underneath yourself to send yourself flying without taking any damage.
	Rank 10: Enemies colliding with walls or each other mid-flight take an additional 600 physical damage. (Note: This is half of the capped landing damage)
	Requires: Crushing Rocks (Rank 3) or Summon: Rock Golem (Rank 3). Player Level 52.
	Synergy - Crushing Rocks: +4 to pillar radius per rank.
	Synergy - Rockslide: +2.5% damage cap on crash landing.
 
* Geyser
 
	Cause the ground in front of you to violently shake to unearth fissures from the ground, revealing 10 geysers of acid.
	Geysers spit acid for 5 secounds around them in random directions. Each acid particle deals 90 damage in 48 unit radius with 100% poison chance.
	Cost: 150 mana.
	Cooldown: 90 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +35 damage per acid particle.
	Rank 5: Acid damage radius is doubled.
	Rank 10: Summons 50% more geysers.
	Requires: Poison Nova (Rank 2). Player Level 50.
	Synergy - Toxic Trap: 2.5% more damage per rank of Toxic Trap.
	Synergy - Poison Nova: 2.5% more damage per rank of Poison Nova.
	Synergy - Upheaval: For every 2 ranks, 1 additional geyser is summoned.
