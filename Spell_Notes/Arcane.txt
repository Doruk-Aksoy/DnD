ARCANE MAGIC
------------
 
* Arcane Bolt

	Fire a bolt of arcane energy to the nearest enemy within 512 units, dealing 80 magic damage.
	Enemies hit take 15% increased damage from arcane spells for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 7 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.85 seconds.
	Per Rank: +40 damage. Every even rank adds an additional target.
	Rank 5: Gains a 10% chance to grant a Power Charge per enemy hit.
	Rank 10: Has a 30% chance to launch another set of bolts.
	Unlocks: Power Globe, Spectral Blades.
	Synergy - Arcane Barrage: +1 projectiles fired per rank of Arcane Barrage.
	Synergy - Power Globe: 5% more damage per rank of Power Globe.
 
* Holy Light
 
	Fire a holy beam of light. If it hits an ally, heal them for 50. Enemies take 50 magic damage. If the enemy is undead or demonic, they take double damage.
	Heal self if it lands on the ground beneath your feet.
	Cost: 10 mana.
	Cooldown: 6 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 heal, +50 damage.
	Rank 5: Stuns undeads or demons hit for 2 seconds.
	Rank 10: Heals for an additional 5% of the target's max health.
	Unlocks: Death Seeker, Purify.
	Synergy - Death Seeker: Damage increases by 10% per rank of Death Seeker.
	Synergy - Purify: Heal increases by 10% per rank of Purify.
	Synergy - Purge: Heal and damage increase by 5% per rank of Purge.

* Blessing
 
	Target an ally or yourself to heal them for 10% of their missing health.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 15 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +1% heal.
	Rank 5: Heals everyone in a 128 unit radius of the target.
	Rank 10: Healing can overstack up to 25% of maximum health.
	Unlocks: Invigorate, Might.
	Synergy - Holy Light: 2.5% increased healing per rank rank of Holy Light.
 
* Power Globe
 
	Hurl a globe of pure energy towards where you're facing, able to pass through objects but explodes on geometry.
	Cast again to detonate at will. It detonates by itself after 3 seconds. Deals 200 magic damage in a 160 unit radius when detonated.
	Enemies slain with Power globe grant a Power Charge.
	Cost: 25 mana.
	Cooldown: 10 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +125 damage.
	Rank 5: Globe can bounce up to 4 times off of surfaces before detonating.
	Rank 10: Sucks enemies into it before dealing the explosion damage, slowing them by 25% for 5 seconds. This affects enemies in 1.25 times the original radius.
	Unlocks: Disintegrate, Mana Burn.
	Requires: Arcane Bolt (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Arcane Bolt: 5% damage per rank of Arcane Bolt.
	Synergy - Arcane Barrage: 2.5% more damage and +4 radius per rank of Arcane Barrage.
 
* Spectral Blades
 
	Summon six blades of energy surging forward up to 144 units in front of you to damage enemies. Blades rip and do 24 magic damage with a ripcount of 8.
	The blades have random orientations but are always vertical aligned.
	Cost: 25 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: +12 damage per blade. Every two ranks gain +1 ripcount and at every third rank gain +1 blade.
	Rank 5: Blades can travel 25% farther.
	Rank 10: Blades become 20% wider.
	Unlocks: Summon: Familiar, Arcane Trap.
	Requires: Arcane Bolt (Rank 1).
	Synergy - Ethereal Body: 10% damage per rank of Ethereal Body.
 
* Death Seeker
 
	Cast a homing bolt of light to hunt down undead or magic. Deal 150 magic damage with undead and demonic enemies taking double damage.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 12 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +100 damage.
	Rank 5: Enemies hurt have 25% chance to be silenced for 5 seconds.
	Rank 10: Slain enemies rest in peace.
	Unlocks: Divine Judgement, Shadow Split.
	Requires: Holy Light (Rank 1).
	Synergy - Holy Light: 5% more damage per rank of Holy Light.

* Purify
 
	Target an ally or an organic enemy. If an ally is targeted, remove all ailments and curses from them. Enemies take 100 damage, with undeads and demonic enemies taking double damage.
	Cost: 40 mana.
	Cooldown: 24 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +100 damage.
	Rank 5: Demons have 50% chance to be stunned for 2.5 seconds.
	Rank 10: Allies gain 50% movement speed for 1.5 seconds.
	Unlocks: Shadow Split, Purge.
	Requires: Holy Light (Rank 1).
 
* Invigorate
 
	Increase the health regeneration of you or a target ally, armor rating and mana regen by 25% for 5 seconds.
	Cost: NA.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +1.5% added increases to provided stats, +0.5 seconds to duration.
	Rank 5: Also grants 15% increased movement speed.
	Rank 10: Can affect players in 192 unit radius of the target.
	Unlocks: Enchant Weapon, Holy Prayers.
	Requires: Blessing (Rank 1).
 
* Might
 
	PASSIVE SKILL - AURA SKILL
	You or allies' melee attacks gain 10% of their damage as extra magic damage within 256 unit radius.
	Cost: 25% of mana reserved.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: +0.75% additional damage gained.
	Rank 5: +128 unit radius.
	Rank 10: Buff applies to all attacks from melee weapons.
	Unlocks: Blessed Aim, Purity of Elements, Arcanery.
	Requires: Blessing (Rank 1).
	
* Mana Burn
	
	Target an enemy to release an arcane bolt of lightning which deals 100 + 10% of your current mana as magic damage.
	Cost: 25 mana.
	Cooldown: 10 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: +0.5% current mana damage, +50 base damage.
	Rank 5: If targeting an enemy capable of casting spells, recover the mana spent over 4 seconds.
	Rank 10: Enemy hit is silenced for 2.5 seconds. Does not work on Dungeon Bosses.
	Unlocks: Arcane Comet.
	Requires: Power Globe (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Purify: Additional 0.2% current mana damage per rank of Purify.
 
* Disintegrate
 
	Project a ray of destruction from your hands. Deal 90 magic damage every 0.25 seconds in a 192 unit long line. Channeled spell.
	The ray grows in length and thickness over the course of 2.5 seconds of channel, gaining up to 33% on both.
	Spend only half the mana cost while stationary. Can hit up to 5 enemies.
	Cost: 4 mana per 0.25 seconds.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds after stopping the channel.
	Cast Time: 1.25 seconds.
	Per Rank: +45 damage, +16 unit length. At every even rank, +1 enemy hit.
	Rank 5: After the ray is primed, it shreds magic resist of enemies hurt by 2 for each damage instance, up to 20, for 10 seconds.
	Rank 10: 10% more damage dealt for each second channeled up to 100%.
	Unlocks: Arcane Barrage.
	Requires: Power Globe (Rank 3).
	Synergy - Arcane Bolt: 5% more damage per rank of Arcane Bolt.
	Synergy - Arcane Trap: 2.5% more damage and +8 unit length per rank of Arcane Trap.
	Synergy - Arcane Barrage: +12 unit length per rank of Arcane Barrage.
 
* Shadow Split
 
	Project a shadow image of yourself that moves in random directions and taunts nearby enemies to attack it.
	Shadow image has 30% of your maximum health as its maximum health and lasts 10 seconds. Counts as a minion.
	Cost: 45 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +2% additional maximum health transferred to shadow image.
	Rank 5: Duration +10 seconds.
	Rank 10: Clone explodes after dying or expiring, dealing 25% of its max health as magic damage in a 192 unit radius.
	Unlocks: Shadow Twin, Temporal Rift.
	Requires: Death Seeker (Rank 3) or Purify (Rank 3).
	Synergy - Shadow Twin: 5% more health per rank of Shadow Twin.
 
* Summon: Familiar
 
	Summon a familiar that will attack nearby enemies on its own, flying over your shoulder at all times. It can't be attacked by enemies.
	Fires projectiles dealing 60 magic damage in quick succession. Lasts 10 seconds.
	Cost: 40 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +40 damage, +1 second duration.
	Rank 5: Projectiles explode in a 64 unit radius for half damage.
	Rank 10: Projectiles can't be deflected and ignores shields.
	Unlocks: Energized Armor.
	Requires: Spectral Blades (Rank 1).
	Synergy - Holy Light: -1 second cooldown per rank of Holy Light.
 
* Arcane Trap
 
	Mark the ground you stand to place a magical trap. By itself, it acts as a proximity mine.
	If there are other traps placed, they form links with each other. These links act as trip-wires.
	If a trap explodes, they will all trigger one after the other with 0.25 seconds of delay. A trap alone deals 300 magic damage in a 144 unit radius.
	If there are multiple traps, as well as exploding, they also project a black hole in the center of all of them.
	Black hole pulls enemies inwards and deals 150 magic damage per trap involved.
	Black hole radius depends on the average of all distances to the center between the traps.
	Traps won't link if they aren't at least 96 units apart. They won't link if they are farther than 256 units.
	The traps last 30 seconds. Maximum of 4 traps.
	Cost: 45 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +180 trap damage, +90 black hole damage, +4.8 units to maximum trap link distance.
	Rank 5: +1 trap count.
	Rank 10: Traps last an additional 15 seconds.
	Unlocks: Ethereal Body.
	Requires: Spectral Blades (Rank 1).
	Synergy - Power Globe: +12 explosion radius and maximum trap link distance.
 
* Divine Judgement

	After a brief delay of it landing, unleash 8 Holy Light around in all cardinal directions.
	Cost: 36 mana.
	Cooldown: 16 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +125 damage.
	Rank 5: Radius +64 units, -4 seconds cooldown.
	Rank 10: Holy lightning ignores immunities and shields.
	Unlocks: Salvation.
	Requires: Death Seeker (Rank 1).
	Synergy - Death Seeker: 5% more damage per rank of Death Seeker.
 
* Purge
 
	Launch a magical tendril that damages all organic enemies up to 512 units from you.
	Tendril deals 180 damage. If target is a demon, it's stunned for 2.5 seconds. If target is undead, it's silenced for 2.5 seconds.
	Tendril will chain up to three more targets before disappearing. Chaining distance is 160 units.
	Cost: 50 mana.
	Cooldown: 18 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +120 damage. Every even rank gives +1 chain.
	Rank 5: Tendril deals 25% more damage each time it chains.
	Rank 10: Jump radius increased to 256 units.
	Unlocks: Salvation, Summon: Angel.
	Requires: Death Seeker (Rank 1), Purify (Rank 1).
	Synergy - Holy Light: 8% more damage per rank of Holy Light.
	Synergy - Divine Judgement: 4% more damage per rank of Divine Judgement, and +1 chain every even rank.

	Cost: 40 mana every 0.5 seconds.

* Shadow Twin
 
	Summon an identical twin of you made of shadow. It shares the same life, resistances and weapon as you.
	The twin will attempt to fire the weapon you held at the time of summoning every few seconds.
	Twin loses 5% of its health every second and will die out when it runs out of life.
	Twin deals 50% less damage.
	Cost: 200 mana.
	Cooldown: 150 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.

	Rank 5: Twin will instead lose 3% of its health every second.

	Unlocks: Ascension.
	Requires: Shadow Split (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Shadow Split: Loses 0.05% less health per rank of Shadow Split.
	
* Temporal Rift

	Cost: 15% mana reservation.

	Requires: Shadow Split (Rank 3).
 
* Energized Armor

	Cost: 100 mana.

	Angel takes four pet cap.
	Cost: 225 mana.
