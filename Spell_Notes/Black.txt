DARK MAGIC
-----------
 
* Plague
 
	Unleash a plague on the target enemy that does 50 damage every 0.5 seconds for 2.5 seconds.
	If the target dies, the plague spreads to the nearest enemy within 128 units with 10% more damage potency. This can spread to more enemies with Contagion.
	Cost: 10 mana.
	Cooldown: 3 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +10 damage.
	Rank 5: Spread potency becomes 30%.
	Rank 10: Plague can spread up to two enemies at once.
	Unlocks: Dark Wind, Essence Theft.
	Synergy - Implosion: 5% more damage per rank of Implosion.
	Synergy - Finger of Death: 5% more damage per rank of Finger of Death.
 
* Summon: Zombie
 
	Summons a zombie to do your bidding. Has 150 health, 20 - 32 melee damage and last 8 seconds.
	Cost: 8 mana.
	Cooldown: 1 second.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +100 health, +16 damage.
	Rank 5: Zombies attack 50% faster.
	Rank 10: Zombies gain 15% to all resistances.
	Unlocks: Summon: Skeleton.
	Synergy - Summon: Slayer Knight: 5% more minion health per rank of Summon: Slayer Knight.
 
* Nox
 
	CURSE
	When cast, all enemies in 128 radius are blinded. They will be attacking in random directions instead of directly towards you.
	Curse lasts 5 seconds.
	Cost: 16 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: Duration +1 second, +8 radius.
	Rank 5: Enemies become more aggressive while affected by the curse.
	Rank 10: The cursed enemies deal 15% less damage to players.
	Unlocks: Delirium, Temporal Chains.
 
* Temporal Chains
 
	CURSE
	Slows down enemies in 160 unit radius area by 30% for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 24 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Duration +1 second, +8 radius.
	Rank 5: Projectiles of affected enemies are also slowed by half the effect.
	Rank 10: Slow becomes 50% and enemies in the area will be less likely to attack.
	Requires: Nox.
	Unlocks: Weaken.
 
* Dark Wind
 
	Unleash a wind of darkness, doing 64 damage in 80 unit radius as it travels every 0.15 seconds.
	Lasts 4 seconds or until it hits a wall.
	Cost: 15 mana.
	Cooldown: 3 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: +32 damage, +0.5 second duration.
	Rank 5: Bounces off walls.
	Rank 10: Damage radius increases by 30%.
	Unlocks: Finger of Death.
	Requires: Plague (Rank 2)
	Synergy - Plague: 5% more damage per rank of Plague.
	Synergy - Carrion Crows: 5% more damage per rank of Carrion Crows.
 
* Essence Theft
 
	Launch a fast unholy missile that deals 450 magic damage over 3 seconds, and returns 20% of the damage enemy takes as health.
	This can spread to more enemies with Contagion.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 18 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.75 seconds.
	Per Rank: +50 damage, -1.5 seconds cooldown.
	Rank 5: Health ratio increased to 33%.
	Rank 10: Enemy hit is pacified for 3 seconds. (Pacified enemies cannot attack)
	Unlocks: Implosion.
	Requires: Plague (Rank 3).
	Synergy - Finger of Death: 10% more damage per rank of Finger of Death.
 
* Summon: Skeleton
 
	Summon an undead melee warrior to do your bidding. Skeletons have 300 health and do 52 - 64 melee damage.
	They last 16 seconds.
	Cost: 25 mana.
	Cooldown: 4 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per rank: +150 health, +32 damage.
	Rank 5: Skeletons have 25% chance to block the next attack after being hurt.
	Rank 10: Risen skeletons have 30% chance to become Skeleton Mages, doing 10% more damage but with ranged spells instead. They cannot block attacks and have 25% less health.
	Unlocks: Summon: Ghast, Summon: Dark Portal, Skeleton Mastery.
	Requires: Summon: Zombie (Rank 3).
 
* Skeleton Mastery
 
	PASSIVE SKILL
	Skeletons risen are more potent, they become more powerful with each rank.
	Cost: NA.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: 5% more health and 10% more damage on skeletons.
	Rank 5: Skeletons last 50% longer.
	Rank 10: Skeleton Mages can cast a random tier 1 elemental spell you've learned.
	Requires: Summon: Skeleton (Rank 1).
 
* Delirium
 
	CURSE
	Target enemy falls under the influence of psychosis, targeting a random enemy nearby.
	Curse lasts 5 seconds.
	Cost: 36 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Duration +1 second.
	Rank 5: Mana cost reduced by 20%.
	Rank 10: Affected enemy gets 100% damage bonus for the duration.
	Unlocks: Subjugate.
	Requires: Nox (Rank 2).
 
* Weaken
 
	CURSE
	Reduces the damage of enemies in 128 unit radius by 30%. Curse lasts 8 seconds.
	Cost: 48 mana.
	Cooldown: 40 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +1% damage reduction.
	Rank 5: Radius is doubled.
	Rank 10: +4 seconds to duration.
	Unlocks: Pain Amp, Penetrate.
	Requires: Temporal Chains (Rank 1).
 
* Finger of Death
 
	Project a deadly bolt of red lightning towards your direction to deal 600 magic damage.
	Cost: 75 mana.
	Cooldown: 16 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +300 damage, -1 second cooldown.
	Rank 5: Lightning projected damages all enemies in a line instead of only one enemy.
	Rank 10: Ignores enemy resists and immunities.
	Unlocks: Soulstorm, Carrion Crows.
	Requires: Dark Wind (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Death and Decay: 5% more damage per rank of Death and Decay.
	Synergy - Plague: 5% more damage per rank of Plague.
	
* Contagion

	Mark a 192 unit area, causing enemies within it to take 240 magic damage over 5 seconds. If an enemy dies with magic damage over time effects on it, it
	will spread to enemies in the same radius, carrying along the full damage over time effect to them as well.
	Cost: 40 mana.
	Cooldown: 27 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.6 seconds.
	Per Rank: +80 magic damage.
	Rank 5: Radius increased by 20%.
	Rank 10: Makes any damage over time carried by this ignore shields and fortification.
	Requires: Essence Theft (Rank 3).
	Synergy - Essence Theft: +8 radius per rank of Essence Theft.
 
* Implosion
 
	Launches a sharp needle in front of you. Enemy that comes into contact with it takes 10% of their current health as magic damage.
	This spell cannot kill enemies and will leave them with a minimum of 1% of their health.
	Cost: 125 mana.
	Cooldown: 50 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +1.5% health loss.
	Rank 5: Needle travels 50% faster.
	Rank 10: Needle leeches 2% of the damage as mana.
	Unlocks: Carrion Crows, Nether Walk.
	Requires: Essence Theft (Rank 2).
 
* Summon: Dark Portal
 
	A portal to aid summoning actions. It will increase the potency of your summons created when you cast them near the portal.
	Portal lasts 10 seconds. Portal has 300 health but does not count towards pet cap, however it still benefits from minion modifiers.
	Minions summoned within 256 unit radius of the portal gain 10% more health, duration and damage.
	Cost: 64 mana.
	Cooldown: 120 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: Minions have +10% more health and damage, +1 second duration.
	Rank 5: Minions gain 25% extra movement speed as well.
	Rank 10: Minions gain 25% to all resistances as well.
	Unlocks: Summon Mastery.
	Requires: Summon: Skeleton (Rank 2).
 
* Summon: Ghast
 
	Summons a ghostly minion to cast Dark Wind for you at the same level as your rank. 
	Can shoot maximum of two Dark Winds before having to wait 8 seconds.
	Ghast takes two pet cap. Ghast has 100 health.
	Cost: 65 mana.
	Cooldown: 40 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +100 health.
	Rank 5: Ghast gains the ability to shift in and out of existence when hurt, becoming untargetable for 0.5 seconds, with a cooldown of 3 seconds.
	Rank 10: Ghast wait time between attacks reduced to 5 seconds.
	Unlocks: Summon: Slayer Knight.
	Requires: Summon: Skeleton (Rank 3).
 
* Subjugate
 
	CURSE
	Launch a ball to curse an enemy on impact, completely pacifying them. They will not retaliate for 8 seconds.
	Duration is halved on bosses. Does not work against Dungeon Bosses.
	Cost: 80 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Duration +1 second.
	Rank 5: Affects 128 units around the target as well.
	Rank 10: Has 50% chance to convert the affected enemies to your side as temporary minions for the duration.
	Unlocks: Transmogrify.
	Requires: Delirium (Rank 3).
 
* Penetrate
 
	CURSE
	Allows one to magically penetrate imbued skin of monsters, allowing projectiles to rip through them.
	Curse lasts 5 seconds. Affects enemies in a 160 unit radius.
	Cost: 60 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Duration +1 second, +8 radius.
	Rank 5: Radius is doubled.
	Rank 10: Attacks or spells that go through afflicted enemies do 25% more damage.
	Unlocks: Remove Resist.
	Requires: Weaken (Rank 2).
 
* Pain Amp
 
	CURSE
	Curse enemies in a 128 unit radius to take 25% more damage for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 90 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +2.5% more damage taken.
	Rank 5: Radius is doubled.
	Rank 10: +5 seconds to duration.
	Unlocks: Life Tap.
	Requires: Weaken (Rank 1).
 
* Soulstorm
 
	After a brief delay, create a storm that deals 100 damage, plus 2 per Soul you have, every 0.35 seconds for 8 seconds.
	Each blast drains 5 Souls, and the next blast counts the Souls left. The storm travels towards you from the target location.
	Recast while it rages (free, past its cooldown, not while silenced) to send it slowly towards where you point.
	Damage is most in the center in a 160 unit radius, lowest at 320 unit radius.
	Cost: 125 mana.
	Cooldown: 75 seconds.
	Cast Time: 4 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 damage, +1 damage per soul every 2 ranks, +0.5 seconds duration (damage on the rank curve).
	Rank 5: Soulstorm area of effect increased by 50%.
	Rank 10: Each blast drains only 3 Souls.
	Requires: Finger of Death (Rank 2).
	Unlocks: Death and Decay.
	Synergy - Dark Wind: +8 radius per rank of Dark Wind.
 
* Carrion Crows
 
	Summon a tornado of 5 flesh eating undead crows around you that follow you around, occasionally leaving you to tear nearby enemies to shreds.
	Crows do 25 damage in very short intervals.
	Cost: 100 mana.
	Cooldown: 40 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +10 damage.
	Rank 5: Crow speed increased by 25%.
	Rank 10: Crows heal you for 5% of the damage they do.
	Requires: Finger of Death (Rank 3), Implosion (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Dark Wind: +1 crow count per two ranks of Dark Wind.
 
* Nether Walk
 
	Travel towards where you're looking, becoming unhittable for the duration.
	While moving through, deal 225 damage to enemies.
	Cost: 75 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +125 damage.
	Rank 5: Energy shield recovery starts immediately on cast if it hasn't already.
	Rank 10: Create an explosion for the same damage where you are at and where you'll end up in a 256 unit radius.
	Requires: Implosion (Rank 2).
 
* Summon Mastery
 
	PASSIVE SKILL
	Your summons gain increased durability.
	Cost: NA.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Duration +1 second, health +2.5%, damage +2.5% to all summons.
	Rank 5: Pets gain 10% to all resistances.
	Rank 10: Pets gain "Hardened Skin" property.
	Requires: Summon: Dark Portal (Rank 1).
 
* Summon: Slayer Knight
 
	Summons an undead slayer knight to do your bidding. Slayer Knights have 1000 health.
	Slayer Knights have two attacks: Melee with 72 - 96 physical damage or ranged with spread of 6 dark flames to deal 60 fire damage per projectile in a 96 unit radius.
	Slayer Knights take three pet cap each.
	Cost: 100 mana.
	Cooldown: 36 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +350 health, +40 melee damage, +30 ranged damage.
	Rank 5: Slayer Knights gain Counter Attack. (When hurt, immediate retaliation with short invulnerability period)
	Rank 10: Slayer Knight melee becomes an AoE slashing wave attack.
	Unlocks: Summon: Doomguard.
	Requires: Summon: Ghast (Rank 1).
 
* Transmogrify
 
	CURSE
	Launch a cursed fireball to reduce any monster class lower than a Baron to a random pickup.
	Cost: 200 mana.
	Cooldown: 90 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: -5 mana cost, -3 second cooldown.
	Rank 5: 25% increased chance to drop health pickups.
	Rank 10: Unaffected enemies gain -10 to all resistances for 10 seconds.
	Unlocks: Banish.
	Requires: Subjugate (Rank 3).
 
* Remove Resist
 
	CURSE
	Target a nearby monster to remove any resist traits they have for 3 seconds.
	Cost: 90 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +1 duration.
	Rank 5: Can affect enemies in a 128 unit radius of the primary target as well.
	Rank 10: Curse also makes the monster take 25% extra damage from the damage types they have resistances to for the duration.
	Unlocks: Break Immunity.
	Requires: Penetrate.
 
* Life Tap
 
	CURSE
	Launch a ball of curse to affect enemies in a 160 unit radius.
	These enemies have a chance to heal you for 10 health when you hit them.
	They will heal for 2.5% of missing health when they die. Curse lasts 10 seconds.
	Cost: 100 mana.
	Cooldown: 60 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +10 heal on hit.
	Rank 5: Heals for 5% of missing health instead.
	Rank 10: Radius is doubled.
	Unlocks: Silence.
	Requires: Pain Amp (Rank 1).
 
* Death and Decay
 
	Channel for up to 10 seconds in a target location to create a zone of pure death.
	Enemies take 5% of their life as magic damage every second. You are slowed by 33% while channeling.
	Zone radius is 192 unit radius. Bosses take half damage.
	Cost: 125 mana every second until the duration.
	Cooldown: 90 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +0.25% more life lost.
	Rank 5: Radius is doubled.
	Rank 10: Cooldown is halved.
 
* Summon: Doomguard
 
	Summon a mighty demon from the deepest pits of hell. Doomguard has 2000 health.
	Doomguard can melee for 250 damage in an arc, has a ranged meteor attack for 200 on hit and 300 damage in a 160 unit radius.
	Doomguard takes five pet cap instead of the regular one cap.
	Cost: 275 mana.
	Cooldown: 120 seconds.
	Cast Time: 4 seconds.
	Per Rank: Health +1250, melee damage +100, ranged damage +80 on direct and 100 on radius.
	Rank 5: Doomguard can cast War Stomp, stunning nearby enemies for 2.5 seconds and dealing its melee damage to enemies.
	Rank 10: Doomguard can cast a random curse you've learned once every 30 seconds.
	Requires: Summon: Slayer (Rank 1).
 
* Banish
 
	CURSE
	Fires a cursed ball which temporarily removes the enemy hit from this realm for 3 seconds.
	Cost: 200 mana.
	Cooldown: 60 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +1 duration.
	Rank 5: Target is afflicted by a random curse you've learned once they reappear.
	Rank 10: Gain a random attribute of the enemy (if applicable) when they reappear for half the duration.
	Requires: Transmogrify (Rank 1).
 
* Break Immunity
 
	CURSE
	Completely removes a random immunity from the target enemy for 15 seconds, if applicable. Otherwise does nothing.
	Cost: 300 mana.
	Cooldown: 160 seconds.
	Cast Time: 4 seconds.
	Per Rank: -4 second cooldown.
	Rank 5: Can affect enemies in a 192 unit radius.
	Rank 10: Removes all immunities instead of a random one.
	Requires: Remove Resist (Rank 3).
 
* Silence
 
	CURSE
	Renders a target monster unable to use their spells for 3 seconds.
	Cost: 360 mana.
	Cooldown: 150 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: Duration +0.5 seconds, cooldown -5 seconds.
	Rank 5: Mana cost -100.
	Rank 10: Can affect enemies in a 192 unit radius.
	Requires: Life Tap (Rank 2).
