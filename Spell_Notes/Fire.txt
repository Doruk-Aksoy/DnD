FIRE
----
 
* Blaze
 
	Sets target enemy on fire. Enemy takes fire damage over 3 seconds for 8 damage every 0.25 seconds.
	Cost: 8 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank:  Adds 4 damage to tics, cooldown drops by 0.25 seconds.
	Rank 5: Adds 1 more second.
	Rank 10: Blaze affects enemies in 96 unit radius of the primary target.
	Unlocks: Infernal Strike, Warmth.
	
* Fireball

	Throw a ball of fire that deals 100 damage on impact.
	Cost: 5 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 damage, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: An additional fireball to the side is cast.
	Rank 10: An additional fireball to the side is cast.
	Unlocks: Infernal Strike, Pyroblast.
 
* Infernal Strike
 
	Your next melee attack deals 50 additional fire damage. Has 25% chance to affect a 128 unit radius.
	Cost: 6 mana.
	Cooldown: 3 seconds.
	Cast Time: Instant.
	Per Rank: Adds 25 damage, cooldown drops by 0.75 seconds, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: +25% chance to affect area.
	Rank 10: Guarantees a crit.
	Requires: Blaze (Rank 2) or Fireball (Rank 2). Player level 8.
	Unlocks: Immolation.
 
* Warmth
 
	Increases mana regeneration by 30% for the next 5 seconds.
	Cost: 10 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +0.5 flat mana regen.
	Rank 5: Duration becomes 10 seconds.
	Rank 10: When cast, allies in a 192 unit radius are also affected.
	Unlocks: Incinerate.
	Requires: Blaze (Rank 4) or Fireball (Rank 4). Player level 8.
	
* Heat Shield

	Covers the targeted ally or your armor in fire, causing enemies hit to get ignited while granting 100 armor rating for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 10 mana.
	Cooldown: 3 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +1 second duration and 25 added armor rating, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Ignites from this last 100% longer.
	Rank 10: Ignites from this are guaranteed to spread.
	Requires: Warmth. Player level 16.
 
* Incinerate
 
	PASSIVE SKILL
	Your fire damage has 30% chance to cause enemies to explode on death, doing 5% of their max health as damage around 128 unit radius.
	Cost: NA.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Explosion damage increases by 0.25%.
	Rank 5: Radius increases to 192.
	Rank 10: No longer does self damage.
	Requires: Warmth (Rank 3). Player level 16.
	
* Boiling Blood

	Grant yourself a buff that gives 25% increased movement speed and 15% cooldown recovery rate for 10 seconds, but losing 5 health per second.
	Cost: NA.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: 1% additional movement speed and 1 health lost per second.
	Rank 5: +2 seconds duration.
	Rank 10: 5% additional cooldown recovery rate.
	Requires: Incinerate, Fire Jet or Heat Shield. (Any of them rank 1). Player level 24.
	
* Anger

	PASSIVE SKILL - AURA SKILL
	Casts an aura that grants all nearby allies and yourself 15% more fire damage as well as 10% base ignite chance in 256 unit radius.
	Cost: 30% mana reserved.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: 1% more fire damage, 8 unit radius.
	Rank 5: +5% additional ignite chance.
	Rank 10: Ignites on you and affected allies last 50% less.
	Requires: Incinerate, Fire Jet or Heat Shield. (Any of them rank 1) Player level 24.
 
* Pyroblast
 
	Hurl a ball of flame towards where you're facing. Deals 250 damage on impact around 108 radius.
	Cost: 12 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Damage increases by 32, +1.8 mana cost.
	Rank 5: No longer damages you.
	Rank 10: Explosion causes additional, smaller fireballs to erupt. They do 1/8th damage of pyroblast.
	Requires: Blaze (Rank 4) or Fireball (Rank 4). Player level 16.
	Unlocks: Searing Bond, Scorching Ray, Flammability.
	Synergy - Blaze: 5% more damage per Blaze rank.
	Synergy - Fireball: 5% more radius per Fireball rank.
 
* Searing Bond
 
	Lash out a chain to immobilize an enemy. Does 75 damage and snares enemy for 1.5 seconds.
	Cost: 18 mana.
	Cooldown: 15 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +0.25 duration, +25 damage, +1.2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Duration increases by 2 seconds.
	Rank 10: Also affects enemies in a 160 unit radius of the primary target.
	Requires: Pyroblast (Rank 3). Player level 24.
	Unlocks: Immolation.
	
* Scorching Ray

	Project a beam of fire dealing 80 damage every 0.2 seconds in front of you. The beam applies Fire Exposure after hitting a target for 2.5 seconds, giving -25%
	to fire resistance for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 3 mana every 0.2 seconds.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.35 seconds.
	Per Rank: Beam length +8 units, +8 damage, +0.333 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Beam Width +25%.
	Rank 10: Beam can go through all enemies.
	Requires: Pyroblast (Rank 2).  Player level 24.
	
* Flammability

	CURSE
	Curses enemies in a targeted area around 256 units, causing them to have -30% to fire resistance for 8 seconds. They also gain 25% base chance to be ignited.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: None.
	Per Rank: -1 additional fire resistance reduction, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: 25% increased area of effect.
	Rank 10: +4 seconds to duration.
	Requires: Pyroblast.  Player level 30.
 
* Immolation
 
	Create an aura of fire in 96 units around you that constantly deals 64 fire damage every second.
	Cost: 10 mana per second.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds after turning off.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Damage increases by 16, damage radius by 8, +1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Mana cost reduced by 4.
	Rank 10: Damage applies Blaze.
	Requires: Infernal Strike, Searing Bonds. Player level 36.
	Synergy - Infernal Strike: 5% more damage per Infernal Strike rank.
	
* Rain of Fire

	Mark a target location to rain comets around 320 unit radius dealing 35 damage in a 72 unit radius for 4 seconds.
	Cost: 25 mana.
	Cooldown: 10 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: +8 damage on comets and +8 effect area, +2.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Rains comets 20% faster.
	Rank 10: Can hold two charges, each requiring their own cooldown.
	Requires: Immolation. Player level 40.
	Synergy: Volcano: +1 additional comet per wave per Volcano rank.
 
* Flame Pillar
 
	Creates a pillar of flame going forth 30 units per tic for 2 seconds. Does 24 damage each tic for 12 tics in 96 unit radius.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: 12 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Damage goes up by 8, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Ignores shields.
	Rank 10: Creates two pillars side by side instead of one.
	Unlocks: Molten Boulder, Fire Demon.
	Requires: Player level 36.
	Synergy - Blaze: 2.5% more damage per Blaze rank.
	Synergy - Fireball: 5% more radius per Fireball rank.
	Synergy - Pyroblast: 2.5% more damage per Pyroblast rank.
 
* Fire Jet
 
	Shoots jets of fire that spread sideways dealing 160 on impact and 24 damage on sideways.
	Cost: 15 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: Direct hit damage +40, sideways damage +6, +1.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Direct hit explodes around itself in a 128 unit radius for half its damage.
	Rank 10: Sideways flames rip.
	Requires: Warmth (Rank 3).  Player level 16.
	Synergy - Blaze: 2.5% more damage per Blaze rank.
	Synergy - Fireball: 2.55% more damage per Fireball rank.
	Synergy - Pyroblast: 5% more damage per Pyroblast rank.
 
* Summon: Fire Demon
 
	Summons a fire demon to fight for you for 20 seconds. Demon has 350 health. Can shoot fireballs or set the ground on fire.
	Cost: 40 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: Health +50. Damage of monster +10%, +2.5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Lasts 10 more seconds.
	Rank 10: Can cast flame pillar same level as your flame pillar.
	Requires: Flame Pillar (Rank 3). Player level 45.
	Synergy - Warmth: 2.5% more health per Warmth rank.
 
* Molten Boulder
 
	Calls down a giant meteor from the sky to crash on enemies. Boulder does 24 damage as it travels and 256 on impact.
	It will shatter on impact of a wall or after 6 seconds.
	Cost: 60 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 4 seconds.
	Per Rank: Travel damage +6, impact +48, +4 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Lasts 3 more seconds.
	Rank 10: Sends 6 Pyroblasts around it after shattering.
	Requires: Flame Pillar (Rank 2). Player level 45.
	Synergy - Blaze: 2.5% more travel damage per Blaze rank.
	Synergy - Fireball: 2.5% more impact damage per Fireball rank.
	Synergy - Pyroblast: 2.5% more damage per Pyroblast rank.
	
* Righteous Fire

	Engulf yourself in flames, burning for 50% of your health and energy shield as base fire damage in 128 unit radius per second. Grants you a buff that gives
	20% more spell damage.
	Cost: None.
	Cooldown: 1 second.
	Cast Time: None.
	Per Rank: 2% additional health damage and 8 units radius.
	Rank 5: You are treated with additional 25% fire resistance towards this spell.
	Rank 10: Cannot be chilled, frozen or ignited while burning from this spell.
	Requires: Flame Pillar (Rank 2). Player level 40.
	Synergy - Immolation: 8 unit radius per Immolation rank.
 
* Annihilus
 
	Channel for the cast time to create an immense explosion at target location, up to 512 units away. Can cast again to terminate early for less damage.
	250 damage in a +96 radius per 0.5 seconds channeled. You are slowed down by 50% while channeling. Maximum of 2.5 seconds of channel.
	Cost: 50 mana per 0.5 seconds channeled.
	Cooldown: 50 seconds.
	Cast Time: Minimum of 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: +25 damage per 0.5 seconds channeled, +5 mana cost.
	Rank 5: No longer hurts the caster.
	Rank 10: Max target range doubled to 1024.
	Requires: Molten Boulder (Rank 4). Player level 52.
	Synergy - Molten Boulder: 5% faster channel time per Molten Boulder rank.
	
* Volcano

	Conjure a miniature volcano at a target location, bursting with molten rocks and lava periodically for 10 seconds. Each bit deals 40 damage in 80
	unit radius.
	Cost: 100 mana.
	Cooldown: 35 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +0.5 duration, +15 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Bursting bits deal 30% increased damage.
	Rank 10: Creates 25% more lava and rocks.
	Requires: Immolation or Righteous Fire. Player level 52.
