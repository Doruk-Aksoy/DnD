LIGHTNING
---------
 
* Charged Bolt
 
	Launch a bolt that traverses randomly towards where you were facing, bouncing off of surfaces. Deals 10-120 damage. Lasts 3 seconds.
	Cost: 4 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: Max damage +15. At every even rank gains +1 to bolts created.
	Rank 5: Duration increases to 5 seconds.
	Rank 10: Bolts can bounce off of enemies.
	Unlocks: Lightning Spear, Shock.
	Synergy - Lightning Spear: 25% damage per rank of Charged Bolt.
	Synergy - Chain Lightning: +1 bolt count per rank of Chain Lightning.
 
* Spark
 
	Zap a small area up to 128 units in front of you for 1-30 damage in a 96 unit radius every 0.15 seconds for 1.5 seconds.
	Cost: 6 mana.
	Cooldown: 1.8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.65 seconds.
	Per Rank: Max damage +15.
	Rank 5: Area of effect becomes 160.
	Rank 10: Zap duration becomes 3 seconds.
	Unlocks: Telekinesis, Static Field.
 
* Lightning Spear
 
	Throw a spear of lightning that deals 15-90 damage.
	Bounces around for 6 times and deals 2-40 damage per bounce in a 72 unit radius.
	If it comes into contact with an enemy, it will attempt to pass through 3 times.
	Cost: 16 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: Damage +25. Every even rank gain +1 bounce.
	Rank 5: Pass attempts +2.
	Rank 10: Bounce damage also applies on pass attempts.
	Unlocks: Forked Lightning.
	Requires: Charged Bolt (Rank 2), Player level 12.
	Synergy - Charged Bolt: 2.5% damage per rank of Charged Bolt.
	Synergy - Forked Lightning: 5% damage per rank of Forked Lightning.
	Synergy - Chain Lightning: +1 pass attempt per rank of Chain Lightning.
 
* Shock
 
	Electrocute a target enemy to increase the lightning damage they take by 25% for 5 seconds.
	Cost: 24 mana.
	Cooldown: 15 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: Damage amp +2.5%.
	Rank 5: Duration increases to 8 seconds.
	Rank 10: Debuff instead applies to all elemental damage instead of only lightning damage.
	Unlocks: Blink.
	Requires: Charged Bolt (Rank 3), Player Level 10.
 
* Telekinesis
 
	Pushes a target enemy within 640 units away from you using magnetic power.
	Cost: 18 mana.
	Cooldown: 20 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: Push distance increases by 10%, cooldown -0.5 seconds.
	Rank 5: Pushed enemy is stunned for 1 second.
	Rank 10: Pushed enemy takes 10% of it's maximum health as lightning damage.
	Unlocks: Blink.
	Requires: Spark (Rank 2), Player Level 8.
 
* Static Field
 
	PASSIVE SKILL - AURA SKILL
	Create an aura around you that periodically zaps a random enemy in a 128 unit radius for 75 damage.
	Zap period is 5 seconds.
	Cost: 25% mana reserved.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: Damage +15, radius +16.
	Rank 5: Zap period lowers to 2.5 seconds.
	Rank 10: Zaps apply Shock.
	Unlocks: Overcharge.
	Requires: Spark (Rank 3), Player Level 12.
	Synergy - Spark: +8 radius per rank of Spark.
 
* Forked Lightning
 
	Create a lightning bolt to the enemy you're facing dealing 15-325 damage. Forks up to 5 enemies behind initial target.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 7 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: Maximum damage +75. At every even rank gains +1 fork.
	Rank 5: Cooldown -3 seconds.
	Rank 10: +5 to fork count.
	Unlocks: Chain Lightning.
	Requires: Lightning Spear (Rank 4)
	Synergy - Charged Bolt: 5% more damage per rank of Charged Bolt.
	Synergy - Chain Lightning: +1 fork every 2 levels of Chain Lightning.
	
* Crackling Lance (Very similar to PoE, needs a cylindrical lightning tube for the beam)

	Release a beam that deals lightning damage in a long area in front of you, with several smaller beams attached to it along.
	The spell gains intensity the more its cast without moving or pause, with the branching decreasing by 33% per intensity.
	Intensity grants 10% more length to the beam and 35% more damage per stack, up to 3. Initial damage is 60-240.
	Cost: 20 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: Max damage +60.
	Rank 5: Beam width increases by 20%.
	Rank 10: Gain +1 maximum intensity stack.
	Unlocks:
	Requires: Lightning Spear (Rank 2), Shock (Rank 2)
	Synergy - Lightning Spear: 5% more damage per rank.
	Synergy - Overcharge: Intensity stacks last 0.15 seconds after moving or not casting.
 
* Blink
 
	Dash towards a 96 unit distance.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 10 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1 second.
	Per Rank: -0.5 cooldown, +16 distance.
	Rank 5: No cast time.
	Rank 10: Free to cast.
	Unlocks: Monsoon.
	Requires: Shock (Rank 2), Telekinesis (Rank 2)
 
* Overcharge
 
	Initiate a charge up period of 3 seconds where you build up electric charge based on your speed.
	After 3 seconds, charging will stop and your next attack will unleash it.
	Deal 25 damage per charge. Maximum 200 charges can be accumulated.
	Cost: 32 mana.
	Cooldown: 16 seconds.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: +5 damage per charge.
	Rank 5: Charge generation rate +25%.
	Rank 10: Charges can be accumulated for 2 more seconds.
	Requires: Static Field (Rank 1), Player Level 16.
	
* Energy Blade

	Gain added lightning damage for melee weapons based on 25% of your energy shield. Buff causes you to have 50% less energy shield.
	Minimum added lightning damage is 2% of the energy shield, whereas the maximum is 25%.
	Cost: NA.
	Cooldown: 8 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.5 seconds.
	Per Rank: 1% additional energy shield scaling on maximum damage.
	Rank 5: Minimum energy shield scaling becomes 5%.
	Rank 10: Buff instead causes only 40% less energy shield.
	Requires: Static Field (Rank 2), Player Level 30.
	
* Conductivity

	CURSE
	Curses enemies in a targeted area around 256 units, causing them to have -30% to lightning resistance for 8 seconds. They also gain 25% base chance to be overloaded.
	Cost: 30 mana.
	Cooldown: 5 seconds.
	Cast Time: None.
	Per Rank: -1 additional lightning resistance reduction, +2 mana cost.
	Rank 5: 25% increased area of effect.
	Rank 10: +4 seconds to duration.
	Requires: Lightning Spear (Rank 4).  Player level 30.
 
* Chain Lightning
 
	Unleash a bolt of lightning that upon hitting an enemy, will travel to next nearest enemy.
	Deals 400 damage on the first enemy, lowered by 5% per chain for at most 75% reduction.
	Chains 5 times.
	Cost: 50 mana.
	Cooldown: 15 seconds.
	Cast Time: 1.25 seconds.
	Per Rank: Max damage +80. At every even rank +1 chain.
	Rank 5: Chains instead lose 4% damage per chain instead.
	Rank 10: Gains 30% chance to apply Shock per hit.
	Requires: Forked Lightning (Rank 2). Player Level 32.
	Synergy - Charged Bolt: 5% more damage per rank of Charged Bolt.
	Synergy - Forked Lightning: +1 chain every 2 levels of Forked Lightning.
	
* Storm Brand

	Create a magical brand that can attach to an enemy within 96 unit radius, sending beams up to 3 additional enemies within 128 unit radius as well as the attached enemy.
	Activates every 0.5 seconds. Beams deal 9-90 damage. The branded enemy takes 80% more damage. Increases and reductions to spell haste also apply to activation frequency.
	Brand stays active for 6 seconds. Maximum 1 such brand can be attached on an enemy.
	Cost: 40 mana.
	Cooldown: 4 seconds.
	Cast Time: 0.75 seconds.
	Per Rank: Max damage +18.
	Rank 5: Sends beams to an additional enemy while attached.
	Rank 10: Enemies can have 2 brands of this type attached.
	Requires: Forked Lightning (Rank 2) or Crackling Lance (Rank 2). Player level 35.
	Synergy - Crackling Lance: 2.5% more damage per rank.
	Synergy - Chain Lightning: Can send beams to an additional enemy per 5 ranks of Chain Lightning.
	
* Wrath

	PASSIVE SKILL - AURA SKILL
	Casts an aura that grants all nearby allies and yourself 15% more lightning damage as well as 10% base overload chance in 256 unit radius.
	Cost: 30% mana reserved.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: 1% more lightning damage, 8 unit radius.
	Rank 5: +5% additional overload chance.
	Rank 10: Overload on you and affected allies last 50% less.
	Requires: Telekinesis (Rank 2) or Static Field (Rank 2). Player level 24.
	
* Jolt

	Grant yourself a jolt of energy, instantly recovering the cooldown of the most recently used spell.
	Cost: 45 mana.
	Cooldown: 30 seconds.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: -1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: -5 cooldown.
	Rank 10: Grants 25% spell haste for 5 seconds on use.
	Requires: Shock (Rank 3), Player Level 32.
 
* Monsoon
 
	After a second of delay, clouds gather to electrocute enemies in a 320 unit radius.
	Calls lightning down on enemies that deals 350 damage in an 80 unit radius around the enemy struck.
	Enemies will be struck at random intervals ranging from 0.15 to 0.5 seconds. Monsoon lasts 10 seconds.
	Cost: 75 mana.
	Cooldown: 40 seconds.
	Cast Time: 2 seconds.
	Per Rank: +100 damage.
	Rank 5: Monsoon lasts 5 more seconds.
	Rank 10: Monsoon applies Shock.
	Requires: Blink (Rank 2). Player Level 30.
	Synergy - Static Field: +16 radius per rank of Static Field.
	Synergy - Charged Bolt: 5% damage per rank of Charged Bolt.
 
* Thunder Clap
 
	Slam the ground to create an expanding ring of shockwave around you in 192 unit radius. Expands 3 times with 30% more radius each time.
	Slam does 180 plus 40% of equipped melee weapon's damage, converted to lightning. Each expansion of the ring adds 30% more damage.
	Cost: 80 mana.
	Cooldown: 25 seconds.
	Cast Time: 3 seconds.
	Per Rank: +90 damage.
	Rank 5: Enemies affected by the clap are stunned for 1.5 seconds.
	Rank 10: Expands for an additional ring.
	Requires: Overcharge (Rank 2).
	Synergy - Static Field: +16 radius per rank.
	Synergy - Spark: 5% more damage per rank.
	
* Storm Call
	
	Mark an area of 192 unit radius on the ground for lightning to strike after 1.6 seconds, dealing 80-720 damage. When lightning strikes, any other
	pending Storm Calls will explode in order after a brief delay.
	Cost: 60 mana.
	Cooldown: NA.
	Cast Time: 0.4 seconds.
	Per Rank: Max damage +180.
	Rank 5: Radius increases to 320.
	Rank 10: Gains a 25% chance for lightning to immediately strike instead of waiting.
	Requires: Crackling Lance (Rank 2). Player Level 40.
	Synergy - Charged Bolt: +8 unit radius per rank.
	Synergy - Blink: 1% chance for lightning to immediately strike instead of waiting per rank.
	
* Surgecast

	The next spell you cast that has cast time will have no cast time and has 20% chance to recover the mana cost spent.
	Cost: 60 mana.
	Cooldown: 45 seconds.
	Cast Time: NA.
	Per Rank: -1 mana cost.
	Rank 5: Mana cost recovery chance becomes 40%.
	Rank 10: Has two charges.
	Requires: Player level 42.
