Low Life: Less than or equal to 50% health.
Full Life: At maximum health.
Recently: 4 seconds.
Overkill: Killing blow that did more than 30% of an enemy's maximum health.
Rage: 1% more attack damage per point up to 30. Rapidly goes down to 0 in 3 seconds if no rage is gained.
