- Ramping Assault (3)
	* Every 6th melee hit on the same enemy deals 20% more damage.
	+ -1 hits needed for the bonus damage.
	
- Cranium Bash (3)
	* Blunt melee weapons have 3% chance to stun enemies on melee hit for 1.5 seconds.
	+ 3% additional chance to stun.

- Echoing Strikes (2) [5]
	* Melee attacks have 12.5% chance to splash around for 50% of the damage.
	+ 12.5% chance.
	- Brutal Strikes (1) or Cranium Bash (1)

- Unending Fury (2) [10]
	* Berserk's damage bonus applies to all weapons at 25% effect.
	+ 25% additional factor.
	- Requires Ramping Assault.
	
- Fervent Reach (2) [12]
	* Melee splash effects can occur 20% further.
	+ 20% additional splash distance.
	- Requires Echoing Strikes.
	
- Blademaster (1) [12]
	* Melee damage and range increase also applies to projectiles fired from melee weapons.

- Exhauster (3)
	* Melee hits have 15% chance to slow enemies hit by 20%.
	+ +5% chance to slow.

- Deep Cuts (3)
	* Slashing melee hits have +15% chance to inflict bleeding on hit.
	+ 7.5% bleeding chance.
	
- Quick & Deadly (1) [6]
	* Slashing attacks repeat an additional time and are 33% faster.
	- Requires Deep Cuts.

- Swift & Precise (1) [8]
	* Melee hits grant 10% movement speed per enemy hit up to 100% for 3 seconds.
	
- Flesh Carver (2) [12]
	* Bleed from melee hits deals damage 10% faster.
	+ 10% faster damage.

===============================

- Flash Parry (3)
	* Parrying projectiles releases a shockwave forward for a limited distance, dealing 100 + 5% max health damage to enemies.
	+ 50 base damage and 10% shockwave travel distance.

- Riposte (1) [3]
	* Successful parries reduce subsequent parry costs by 10% up to a maximum of 30% within a 10 second window.
	
- Expose Weakness (2) [8]
	* Reduce enemy resists by 25% on parry damage for 15 seconds.
	+ 15% additional resist reduction.

- Martial Finesse (2) [12]
	* Melee hits on enemies reduce melee cooldowns by 8%.
	+ Additional 8% reduction.
	
- Gratuitous Violence (1) [12]
	* Overkilling enemies grants rage.
