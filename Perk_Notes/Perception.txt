- Munitionist (3)
	* 20% increased ammo gained from pickups.
	+ Additional 15% increased ammo gained.
	
- Plan B (3)
	* After depleting your magazine fully, gain 10% increased movement speed for 3 seconds.
	+ Additional 10% movement speed.
	
- Excess Conversion (2) [6]
	- Excess ammo from pickups is converted to another ammo type for a weapon you own at 15% rate.
	- Conversion rate has an additional 10%.
	
- Fresh Clip (3) [6]
	- Finishing a reload action empowers the next 5 shots to do 15% more damage.
	+ Empower lasts for an additional round.
	- Requires: Munitionist or Plan B.
	
- Lucky Bullet (2) [9]
	- The very last round in your magazine has an additional +2.5% chance to crit and 25% crit multiplier.
	+ Additional 2.5% crit chance and 25% crit multiplier.
	- Requires: Munitionist or Plan B.
	
- Salvager (1) [12]
	* Enemies have 2% chance to drop ammo tokens, giving 10% ammo to a random weapon you own.
	- Requires: Munitionist or Plan B.

	+ 15% additional damage.
	
- Fired up (3)

	+ Additional +2 damage.
	
- Pumped (3)
	* Shotguns have +1 shell capacity.
	+ Additional +1 shell capacity.
	
- Quaker (3)
	* Artillery weapons gain 10% increased radius.
	+ Additional 10% increased radius.
	
- Dark arts (3)
	* Magic weapons deal 10% increased damage.
	+ Additional 15% damage.
	
- Sharpshooter (1) [6]
	* Precision weapons gain up to 100% more damage with distance, capped at 2048 units.
	- Requires: Precise Technique.
	
- Ceaseless Assault (1) [6]
	* Enemies hit by your automatic weapons recently lose 2 to all resistances up to 30.
	- Requires: Fired up.
	
- Blastier Shots (1) [6]
	* Gain an additional pellet on shotgun attacks.
	- Requires: Pumped.
	
- Earthshaker (1) [6]
	* Artillery attacks gain 10% more damage and radius per continuous attack, up to 3 times.
	- Requires: Quaker.
	
- Essence Theft (1) [6]
	* Enemies slain recently by magical attacks grant 2 magic pierce up to 20.

- Shield stealer (1) [6]
